Yokohama CLI v0.3.3 — suggest walkthrough

A few minutes of live play against a bot seat, to see whether the AI suggestion works and whether its answers make sense.

What held up

The search's projections matched reality, every time

The line suggest prints is the sequence it valued the position by. Three times I played its line out move for move and the game landed exactly where the table said it would — the end-of-turn assistant counts (board 2, hand 6), the +4 VP order fulfilment, the 8 VP church total. Nothing drifted.

It finds genuine multi-step combinations

On turn 3 it proposed: place 2 in the Copper Mine → move the President there free → produce copper → fulfil the Britain order for 4 VP → spend both movement rewards. That combo needed the production to land the exact goods the order wanted, and the next best candidate was 36 points behind. On turn 7 it noticed Red had an empty hand and routed to the Employment Agency to refill.

Determinism and the honesty guards are real

Two presses at one position gave a byte-identical screen and the same stream key; a different position gave a different key. It applied nothing, recorded nothing, and did not autosave. The arithmetic in the table sums to the printed total on every screen I checked, and the stage weights visibly slide (VP weight 10 → 17 → 25 across the opening).

What I'd flag

The Laboratory surcharge reads as a reward

The option renders Laboratory: acquire Mining Technology, +2 yen — and then yen went 3 → 1. extra_yen is the board-slot surcharge (slots 3–6 = +1,+1,+2,+2), i.e. a cost, but +2 yen reads as a gain. The arm directly below it in the same function already phrases its cost correctly as 5th+ tech fee: 2 yen.

crates/yokohama-cli/src/describe.rs:316 · same for extra_imported · inherited by the suggest line display.

Production previews are tech-blind

Produce copper (power 3 -> +1 copper, next +2 at power 4) — the log then said Red: +2 copper. Red owned Mining Technology ("whenever you gain one or more Copper: gain 1 additional Copper"). production_preview calls the base reward ladder and never consults the acting player's techs, so the preview under-reports for anyone holding a production tech.

crates/yokohama-cli/src/describe.rs:216 · the engine is correct; only the preview is.

The evaluator is positionally blind

At the opening placement, 36 of 186 candidates tied at the top; at a movement-reward choice, 10 of 29 did. That is structural rather than a bug: the features are aggregate counts (assistants_on_board is a number, not a map), so any placement that does not change this turn's action is invisible to the score. Where the choice actually changes the outcome the margins are decisive (−36, −153, −166) — but the bot has no sense of territory, adjacency, or denying an area to an opponent.

The playable bot is not the good bot

A bot seat plays family-weighted — the 3.2 weighted-random rung. The 3.3 evaluator that produces these suggestions is only reachable through suggest on your own seat. So the strongest thing in the build is currently advisory only; you cannot yet play against it.

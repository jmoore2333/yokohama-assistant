## 0. THE GATE RULING (adopted under the 3.0–3.4 autonomy envelope; clean-eyes-verified SOUND by two independent lenses)

**The question.** 3.1-spec §3.5/L7 flagged the route enumerator as a possible re-map signal; its §4 gate text includes the parenthetical "(no ∏-materialization, no simple-path DFS)". Research delivered an **allocation-free simple-path DFS** that reproduces `enumerate_president_routes` EXACTLY — same `(dest, path)` pairs, same order, same lex-least witness — validated at **79,037 distinct states with 0 divergences under the shipped configuration** (3,587 organic decision points × a 20-cell tech/money matrix + adversarial constructions incl. the 612- and 694-label cells; 469,834 comparisons counting all six prune variants), at **467 allocations vs the reference's 1,687,537 on the adversarial maximum — 3,613× fewer, O(labels) never O(paths), and ~2× headroom against the bound §4-2 hard-asserts** — while worst-case TIME remains O(affordable simple paths).

**The ruling: L7 is DISCHARGED — no re-map.** Grounds, strongest first:

1. **L7's own trigger did not fire.** Its words: 3.1b "must present a correct cheap enumerator of the distinct affordable labels and prove it reproduces the exhaustive label set; if that proves infeasible, STOP … do not silently fall back to the gate-forbidden DFS or a quiet cap." A correct enumerator WAS presented — bit-exact output at 79,037 states, 3,613× fewer allocations, 36–47× less wall clock at the adversarial maximum — and this adoption is neither silent (this section, a map §11 amendment, a guidingpath row, and the executable blocker record all land with the PR) nor a cap (the output is exact; the L2 income-denial label set is fully preserved).
2. **The governing gate never forbade the shape.** Map §5-3.1's gate reads "worst-case latency/allocation bounds recorded and met (retreat decisions servable without materializing the family); factored-surface ↔ exhaustive-list equivalence tests; zero semantic change" — it commits only RETREAT to non-materialization and says nothing about route search shape. Per map §11 the stop signal is a spec wanting something outside its mapped purpose or gate; the compute-once reframe (§3.1) is inside both.
3. **The parent spec's meter pin makes the allocation reading the coherent one.** 3.1-spec §4's parenthetical is a gloss on what the allocation bound was expected to forbid — the day's two measured allocation blowups (∏-materialization ≈1.02 M allocs; the route DFS ≈0.55 M) — and the sentence that follows demotes everything but allocation COUNT to recorded curves. Reading the parenthetical as an independent architectural prohibition would contradict its own sentence; research separated search shape from allocation behavior (the DFS's sin was materialization cost, and that sin is gone).
4. **A standing owner ruling fixes what the output must be.** The 2026-07-20 effective-route ruling establishes the legal route set IS the simple-path set (one canonical representative per effective route); the L2 ruling requires every affordable label to survive. An enumerator of exactly that set, produced cheaply, is the assignment fulfilled — not evaded.
5. **The alternatives are named and rejected, not absent.** (a) Approximate/capped family — re-opens the owner's L2 ruling; owner territory, not proposed. (b) Keep the delegation stub — the Movement decision point keeps paying the whole exhaustive ceiling; worse on every axis. (c) **Rewrite the frozen `enumerate_president_routes` in place** with the same incremental costing (the reference's 1.69 M allocs live entirely in per-node `RouteVector` rebuilds, so the win is available there too) — REJECTED because it retires the live differential oracle that §4-1's ordered-equivalence gate depends on and mutates the very authority `apply_move` checks against; the factored-side port keeps the frozen reference as the standing oracle. The one option consistent with every standing ruling is the one adopted.

**Failsafe discharged:** both clean-eyes lenses were instructed to attack this ruling first and both returned SOUND (verdicts in §9); lens 2 independently re-ran the counterexample, the critic probe (8,776 comparisons / 0 divergences / max 694 labels), and confirmed the difftest compares exact `Vec<(AreaId, Vec<AreaId>)>` equality — pairs AND order AND witness.

**Honest reframing recorded with the ruling:** the route family never needed *laziness* — it is SMALL (max **observed** 694 adversarial / 38 organic; L9: no measured figure is a ceiling). It needed a **cheap computation**. `MovementRoutes` becomes a compute-per-call small family (§3.1) with a gate shaped for it (§4-2) — deliberately DIFFERENT from retreat's serve-one constant bound, stated everywhere the two could be conflated.

## 3. Design

### 3.1 The route enumerator (`game/route_enum.rs` or within `search_actions.rs`; flat mod + pub use)

Port R2's `Prune::None` prototype (scratch `algorithm/src/lib.rs`, ~430 lines, no new deps, no `unsafe`): `RouteGraph` built off `&GameState` (own-assistant subgraph, ascending adjacency, owner masks, canal-toll edge bits), then the allocation-free DFS with incremental `(mask, tolls)` costing, affordability prune, first-encounter-wins per label, one witness path pushed per label. Output: the family's `Vec<Move>` in the reference's exact `(dest, RouteVector)` order via the **ported** comparator (F2) — plus the Station/Tram teleports and `zero_cost_destinations` suppression (F1 contract) and the InHand `PlacePresident` arm, so the factored family is the WHOLE Movement-routes block.

- **Lex-least-witness soundness: a TWO-HALF written proof in the module docs.** (i) Prototype half: ascending-child preorder DFS emits simple paths in strictly increasing lex order (a prefix precedes its extensions; siblings order by the differing hop), hence first-recorded = lex-least. (ii) Reference half: `movement.rs:508`'s compare-and-keep selects lex-least independently of traversal order. Plus the bridging lemma: both searches range over the same candidate set (same `can_step_onto` predicate, same monotone affordability prune, same simple-path constraint), so the two selection rules agree. Backed by a `debug_assert` drift guard on label re-encounter (`new_path > stored_witness`) and a dedicated tie-break fixture (the `l2_tied_free_routes_fixture` pattern).
- **Totality: a PRE-FLIGHT probe, not a mid-build check.** `fits_fixed_arrays(&GameState) -> bool` evaluated over `BoardLayout` + players BEFORE any array write, guarding all FOUR bounds: area count ≤ 18, out-degree ≤ 17, label classes ≤ the fixed bitset, opponent count ≤ 3 owner bits. A state failing the probe (only hand-built fixtures can) **delegates to the frozen exhaustive path** — output-identical by the equivalence guarantee, documented, pinned by a named oversized fixture (a raw-tuple `AreaId(18)` board, with a comment that it deliberately exceeds generation bounds). No panic on any input (Phase-2.9 totality convention).
- **`MovementRoutes` contract re-framed (recorded loudly):** a compute-per-call SMALL family. `iter_family`/`sample_family`/`family_len` on it invoke ONE enumeration each — no cache in 3.1b (L10 carries the per-decision-point arithmetic to 3.3). `family_len` is EXACT but costs one enumeration — documented on the function, contrasted with retreat's closed form and placement's upper bound. The 3.1a serve-one language does NOT transfer: retreat's "5 allocations" stays retreat's gate; routes' gate is §4-2's O(labels) shape; every doc site listing family costs states the three shapes side by side.

### 3.2 `is_move_legal` / `is_president_path_legal` (design A; new module `game/move_legality.rs`)

Per R3's validated prototype: `is_move_legal(&GameState, &Move, &Rules) -> bool` with an exhaustive match over `Move` (no `_` arm — the `family_kind` drift-guard pattern); `is_president_path_legal` = (i) consecutive-hop adjacency + simplicity + no-return-to-start (mirroring the frozen `validate_president_path`, apply_move.rs:375-421 — the ~35 mirrored lines carry a citation comment and the §4-3 drift guards); (ii) `can_step_onto` per hop (via the F8 visibility change, not a copy); (iii) destination `is_possible_action_area_full`; (iv) affordability via `route_fee_vector`; teleport arms (empty path: Station membership / Tram destination rules). **`PathMode { RulesLegal, Canonical }`**: `RulesLegal` accepts any legal witness (the 588 non-canonical witnesses are ACCEPTS); `Canonical` adds the zero-cost-walk suppression check (R3's `zero_cost_walk_exists` BFS). Contract points recorded: **equivalence tests compare `MovePresident` by `(destination, RouteVector)` label, never raw path** (stated at the test site); **`PlaceThreeAreas.placements` must be strictly ascending** (matching `place_combo` and legal_moves.rs:159-195; a permuted-placements candidate is a §4-3 illegal dimension). Delegated families (AreaAction via public `get_legal_moves`, ChoiceResolution via `generate_choice_resolutions`) each get a phase-purity pin. Design B keeps residue-only duty; the A-vs-B table is recorded in the module docs with policy.

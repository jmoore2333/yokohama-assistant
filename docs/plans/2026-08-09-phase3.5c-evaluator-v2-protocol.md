## 4. The iteration loop

**Round structure** (each round is one ledger row: proposals → evidence → screen → verdict):

1. **Propose.** A round's change-set is one or more edits, each anchored to a tension or to R0 evidence, in one of three categories: (i) **re-pricings** of existing terms — magnitudes inside T1's inequality bands, T2's decay shape, T4's early vector (the primary target), T11's track-cost implications; (ii) **the ONE sanctioned feature change** — T3's post-claim filter (`claimed_by`-viewer excluded from `achievement_progress_sum`), decided IN by the orchestrator under the 2026-08-09 proceed authorization (proposal A1.3 marked the include/exclude 🔶 OD-1; the freeze package puts it before the owner at OD-2 regardless), landing RED-first via scenario 17; (iii) **new term admissions** — T5 denial candidates (church/customs majority-race margins; the T9 tech-majority race with the dummy as third contender; achievement-race proximity; **Placement-root value separation** — proposal A2's explicit v2 quality target, the "186 candidates carrying only 4 distinct one-ply values" degeneracy whose sharpening also serves any future architecture re-measurement, ADR §8 item 2), T6 end-timing candidates (clock control, the dropped empty-Port-slot conjunct, terminal-margin awareness), and the T7/T8 money-cost candidate (re-testing R1-7's "uninformative" exclusion in a regime where both major sinks got dearer). **Any admission that changes the feature COUNT moves `Feature::ALL` FIRST** — the silent-omission hazard is documented in-source at its definition — then the drift pin and the goldens.

2. **Evidence ladder** (proposal §4 A3, in force; a candidate advances only on the rung below it):
   - (L1) a regime-correct signal in the R0 marginal table (or a named structural argument for why the table cannot see it — denial terms are opponent-relative and may need constructed probes);
   - (L2) a constrained held-out re-weighting fit on the fixed corpora — **used as evidence for HAND-SET magnitudes, never as shipped machine weights** (proposal A3(2) verbatim; V2's numbers stay hand-set, the fit informs them), with every measured-then-set constant recording its command and value before use. **Hard constraints are never relaxed at any rung:** the one-ply inequalities `core > on_board > excess` in BOTH vectors (L14/T1 — the non-degeneracy condition, not a prediction), and every degeneracy guard in the suite — including map §8 row 18's tie-break non-degeneracy pin, which gets RE-PINNED if a re-pricing changes tie shape (proposal A1.1; ties sit at ~30% of decisions, so a re-pricing is precisely what perturbs them);
   - (L3) scenario counterfactuals — every adopted change has at least one scenario that lands red-then-green against it, and the full suite stays green;
   - (L4) the **arena screen**: candidate table vs incumbent at BOTH rungs — `greedy-v1@cand` vs `greedy-v1@inc` and `lookahead-v1@cand` vs `lookahead-v1@inc` — 2P, N=200 design units, honest driver. **Mechanism: an in-crate screen HARNESS over the public weighted constructors (`GreedyAgent::with_weights`, `LookaheadAgent::with_knobs`), built as W3's opening instrument** — deliberately NOT a weight knob on the runner's contestant grammar, which would contradict the L12 rule the grammar documents (`Contestant` carries no weight knob; a weight change is a version bump, not a cell parameter). Precedent: greedy.rs's re-pricing test and evaluator_gate's helper layer. How versioned @V1 identities are exposed to the arena AFTER the freeze is the five-rung pre-registration's question, not this harness's. **The screen is a REGRESSION NET, not the positive evidence** (that is L1–L3's job): a near-null screen does not block a corpus-justified re-pricing. One honesty limit stated: L1/L2 evidence is measured on INCUMBENT-play corpora, so it is off-policy for the candidate — the screen is the only on-policy check inside a round, which is exactly why the freeze criteria (§7) demand a fresh on-policy cell for the accumulated total.

3. **Screen masters:** each round's screen draws a FRESH OS-entropy master at round open, recorded in the ledger row BEFORE the screen runs — **and is never redrawn after seeing the result** (master-shopping is the failure mode this pre-commitment blocks; a suspected-degenerate master is handled by recording the screen AND the suspicion, then drawing the NEXT round's master fresh as always). Rotation prevents overfitting the iteration to one master's fields, at a stated price: round-to-round screens are mutually incomparable fields — fine for a regression net, and why "accumulation" is only ever cashed out by §7's single fresh cell. (Diagnostic masters remain for reproduction of any specific screen.)

4. **Adoption rule (pre-committed):** adopt the round's change-set iff (a) the lookahead-rung screen interval is NOT entirely below 0.5 — a `RefusedZeroWidth` classifies per the ADR's standing definition: all-won is ABOVE, all-lost is BELOW; (b) all scenarios green and all hard constraints hold; (c) the greedy-rung screen is reported — a greedy interval entirely below 0.40 pauses the round for orchestrator reconsideration (recorded), since greedy is a preserved rung and a large regression there needs weighing even though lookahead is the shipped agent. The 0.40 is HAND-SET with no measured basis, and it triggers a recorded pause rather than a block — stated so it cannot be mistaken for a derived band. Directions accumulate across rounds; individual rounds may be null-ish — the FREEZE criteria (§7) are where the total must show.

5. **Stopping:** iterate until two consecutive rounds adopt nothing, or the accumulated round screens look like they would hold the freeze criteria (criterion 1 itself is only measurable at the close, on a master drawn there) and remaining candidates are marginal, or a budget of six rounds — then take what stands to §7.

4. **Suite integrity:** all scenarios (grown set) green at V2 with margins re-pointed; every hard constraint and degeneracy guard holds; the fifteen-gate roster green as one measured sequence at the freeze PR close.

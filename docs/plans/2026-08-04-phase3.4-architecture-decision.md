> **Definitions.** `classify(cell)` ∈ {ABOVE, BELOW, CONTAINS}: a `Bootstrap` interval with `lo > 0.5` (strict) is ABOVE; `hi < 0.5` is BELOW; anything else — including an endpoint landing exactly on 0.5 (lens 1 S1) — is CONTAINS. A `RefusedZeroWidth` all-won is ABOVE, all-lost is BELOW; constant-interior is CONTAINS **only after the differing-play precondition passes** — the two seatings of ≥90% of units must have produced differing games, checked by terminal-digest inequality as the stated proxy (lens 1 M6 / critic R6: two effectively identical agents produce constant-interior trivially — map §8 row 14's instrument-dead mode); a precondition failure is a harness bug, not an outcome. `NO_UNITS` or any other shape: harness bug.

> **Surfaces, evaluated per arm and per cell in this precedence BEFORE classification (lens 1 M3): harness-bug → completion-failure → null-gate.**
> - **S1 harness bug:** malformed outcome anywhere → halt, investigate, no adjudication.
> - **S2 completion failure:** any game in a cell returning `ArenaError::CapReached` INVALIDATES that cell (never dropped, never silently scored). Attribution: if the arm whose decisions show nonzero `budget_starved_decisions` is the plausible cause, the routing is **UP the pre-registered ladder** — that arm's cells re-run once at the next higher budget with the starving arm labelled (lens 1 M10: in a matched-budget experiment starvation is the likely cause, and "fall to a lower budget" makes it worse); failure at the top of the ladder → the ADR records `completion-failure at matched budgets` as the finding for that comparison. A cell with nonzero starvation and no cap-hit stands but is **never read as a clean matched-budget adjudication** (lens 1 S4 / critic §7.3).
> - **S3 null-gate:** each adjudication master's same-master null control (§5's C1 for arm 1, C1′ for arm 2 — lens 1 M4: a BINDING arm without a null control re-admits the hazard the control exists for) must return **`IntervalOutcome::Bootstrap` whose interval CONTAINS 0.5 with nonzero realized unit variance** — the `arena_gate` null clause's own wording. **Any `RefusedZeroWidth` at a null, or a zero SE, FAILS the gate** (lens 2 M1: v1's "excludes 0.5" was satisfied by exactly the degenerate refusal the control exists to catch). On a failure: triage first (run the capped-S1 self-null on the same master to separate an X-machinery defect from a master excursion — lens 1 N2), then that arm's primary + null re-run ONCE at the pre-declared alternate master, **REPLACING the failed arm's cells** (never augmenting — lens 1 S13; the ~5% false-trigger rate buys one pre-declared extra look, stated); a second failure → harness-defect ticket, and the ADR records "the instrument could not resolve a zero edge on two masters".

> **THE DECISION (after surfaces clear).** Let `J = (classify(C2 arm 1), classify(C2 arm 2))` and `W = classify(C3)` (the wall cell, whose opponent is the SHIPPED `lookahead-v1:b=4` — deliberately, so no adoption can rest solely on capped-S1 comparisons), and let `C0-certified` mean C0's interval ⊆ **[0.44, 0.56]** (an equivalence band of ±0.06 ≈ ±42 Elo, under half the measured ladder rung spacing; N = 200 makes the band satisfiable — a true-null interval spans ≈ ±0.046 — and a miss means the capped arm is not certified as the shipped rung's proxy; lens 1 M9: v1 read non-rejection at N=100 as equivalence and wired C0 to nothing).

> > **MCTS-v1 is ADOPTED as the serious-strength architecture for 3.5 iff `J = (ABOVE, ABOVE)` AND `W = ABOVE` AND C0-certified. In EVERY other post-surface outcome, S1 is RETAINED — with incumbency stated in the ADR as a PRE-REGISTERED DEFAULT, never a merit finding** (lens 1 M1/M2: v1 let the node axis adopt alone with the wall merely printed, contradicting the map's "they can rank differently", and its no-edge tie-break named a quantity the wall-matched cell equalises by construction — both repaired by this single conjunctive rule).

> The named finding per non-adoption outcome (each pre-registered so the ADR writes itself mechanically): `J=(ABOVE,ABOVE)` with `W=CONTAINS` → *per-node superiority at wall parity*, recorded, 3.5 commitment stays with S1 pending a wall-axis disposition (the named cheap follow-up: one re-calibrated C3); `J=(ABOVE,ABOVE)` with `W=BELOW` → **the axes rank differently — the map-anticipated headline finding**, S1 retained on the pre-registered default, the map §1.3 caveat recorded beside it (a wall result on this laptop is an implementation-efficiency fact, not an architecture ceiling — lens 1 N1); `J=(BELOW,BELOW)` → S1 retained on the merits, with W recorded (a `W=ABOVE` there is again the axes-disagree headline); any J containing CONTAINS, or `(ABOVE,BELOW)`/`(BELOW,ABOVE)` → **no robust edge at this N** — the arm-disagreement case is named an accepted consequence (a one-master sweep plus a one-master interior is NOT robust evidence, deliberately — lens 1's §0 answer 1), and the ADR reports the **JOINT detectable edge** (lens 1 M5: the conjunction's operating characteristics are stated, not implied — per-arm power ≈ 0.98 at θ = 0.60 under the measured Var_pair ≤ 0.119 at N = 200, joint ≈ 0.97; the 3.2 policy's 97→200 rounding is what buys the conjunction its power). ## 4. Realized `Var_pair` per cell, and the joint detectable edge recomputed at realized variances

### 4.1 The realized variances

Both divisors are printed by the runner; the sample divisor is used in every inversion below. `ρ` is the implied within-unit correlation, `ρ = 2·Var/(θ̂(1−θ̂)) − 1`, and the near-null column is the self-consistent pair variance that ρ implies at an alternative near 0.5, `(0.25 − δ²)(1+ρ)/2` — precisely the fixed point `V = (0.25 − 7.848878·V/n)(1+ρ)/2`, which reproduces every printed value exactly (plugging the realized-variance δ in does NOT; T9 review N2) — the T7-review distinction between *an outcome statistic* and *the variance of the alternative you want to detect*, applied here cell by cell.

**The §5 caveat is discharged, and in the direction §5 pre-committed.** Spec §5 warned that 0.1225 is a *policy-rung* Var_pair, that C2 is cross-architecture, and that *"the ADR does not call 0.1225 vindicated for C2's alternative until it does"*. It does not: **C2's own realized Var_pair (0.0206 / 0.0251) is an outcome statistic at θ̂ ≈ 0.05, not a near-null variance**, and quoting it as one would understate the detectable edge by a factor of ~2.4 in the flattering direction. ## 7. What this decision does NOT say

Each item is a **condition of the finding**, stated so a future reader does not over-read the headline.

1. **It does not say MCTS is a bad architecture for this game.** It says **MCTS-v1** — this module, at the §2.5 screening-selected knobs, at K=1, at 2P, at total scored-successor budgets of 750/2016/2100, over the frozen `WeightTable::V1`, on these masters — loses to S1 on both matched axes. Architecture families are not adjudicated by one implementation at one budget, and §0.1's own preservation clause (MCTS-v1 stays a labelled diagnostic) is written for exactly that reason.
2. **It is conditional on a knob selected at screening resolution — the addendum's own label, carried here as §4 required.** The twelve screening configurations were separated at N=30, where the per-cell SE ≈ 0.06 exceeds the grid spacing, so `leaf=hand-off · c=0.1 · scale=100` is **partly noise-chosen**. It is also the *exploitation end* of the c axis, and the shipped `c = √2` was the worst c value in both leaf halves. A better-tuned MCTS-v1 is not excluded by this experiment; it is not evidenced by it either.
3. **It is conditional on evaluator v1.** §0.5 freezes `WeightTable::V1` for the whole experiment, so every figure here is **JOINT — architecture × V1**. A tree that must read its leaves through a particular evaluator is being measured together with that evaluator.
4. **It is 2P only.** 3–4P strength cells are OUT of 3.4 (§1) and there is no measured `Var_block` to size them from (map §8 row 16).
5. **It says nothing about candidate (c), information-set variants, or K > 1.** All ran at K = 1 by §0.4's pre-committed grounds; §0.4's routing stays ARMED, and §6.4 above records what the entry instrument does and does not currently measure.
6. **C2's opponent is not the shipped rung, and the qualified wording applies.** C0 is **not** certified — interval [0.190000, 0.272500] against a band of [0.44, 0.56] — so **R1's ladder values may NOT be cited for the capped arm**, and every node-matched claim in this document is to be read as *"against a budget-limited S1 whose C0 read [0.190000, 0.272500]"*. Per §5's scope note the non-certification attaches to the capped arm as a **PACKAGE** (the 750 budget, the value-ordered allocation, and the dice reordering that allocation induces even at a non-binding cap), never to the budget alone. The direction of this qualification is **against** MCTS-v1, not for it — but it is stated as a limit on the instrument regardless, and C3 exists precisely so the decision does not rest on it.
7. **It does not locate MCTS-v1 on the b-ladder.** C5 did not fire; no rung was walked; the "how much weaker, in shipped-rung currency" question has an interval per cell and no ladder position. Master `…_0009` is unspent.
8. **The near-boundary caveat is not waived.** C2's two arms, C3 and C4 all carry the report's own NEAR-BOUNDARY CAVEAT (few distinct unit values), so their intervals are **directional rather than finely calibrated**. The classification depends only on `hi < 0.5`, which is 0.43 away — but the *widths* should not be quoted as precise.
9. **It is not a claim about wall-clock ceilings.** Map §1.3's caveat stands: a wall result on this laptop is an implementation-efficiency fact, not an architecture ceiling. C3's ratio matched *this* implementation of MCTS-v1 against *this* implementation of S1.

## 8. The evidence that would reverse it

Named, re-runnable conditions — what to measure, not what to argue.

1. **Evaluator v2.** The finding is joint with `WeightTable::V1` (`weights=-2162047371061899018`). Weights are first-order (research R1-2: re-weighting moved held-out AUC 0.5646 → 0.6714 while a term-set redesign moved 0.008), and a tree's value at depth is a claim about what its evaluator can see. §0.5's per-controller version rule already says both controllers' figures stop being comparable when weights move. **Re-run:** C2 both arms + C3 at v2, same masters, same N.

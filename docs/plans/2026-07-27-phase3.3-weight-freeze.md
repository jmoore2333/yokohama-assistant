## 0. The one-paragraph result

The freeze protocol ran end to end. Greedy-v1 regenerated the corpus under the hand-set first cut — **2,000 2P self-play games, 326,972 view snapshots, 12,000 (game × seat × tercile) cells** — and every one of the sixteen features was re-measured at EARLY / MID / LATE with game-clustered bootstrap intervals. §2.4's operationalized materiality rule (*a measured sign whose game-clustered 95% CI EXCLUDES the shipped direction*) **fired on nothing**: 29 of the 32 (feature × endpoint) signs came back with their interval wholly on the shipped side of 0.5, and the other three were *no signal*, not contradictions. **The single adjustment pass therefore moved zero weights, and the first cut became `WeightTable::V1` unchanged.** `EVAL_IMPL_VERSION` is bumped 1 → 2 because that const's own rule pre-committed the freeze to a bump; the finer key, `weight_table_identity`, is unmoved at `0xe1fedd5efc49d0f6`, so records on both sides of the bump are behaviourally comparable and the doc says so. Three things the protocol asked for beyond the signs: the church early-negative **survived** the regime change that was supposed to overturn it, the warehouse magnitude **grew**, and the L14 hand-split ordering is **held against a measurement that runs the other way** — with the mechanism identified rather than waved at. The §2.5 recovery share measured 0.000703 and its per-edit floor is set at half.

**An empty adjustment pass is a result, not a skipped step.** The full per-feature adjudication is §3 below so the emptiness is auditable rather than asserted, and §7 names the three places where a stronger rule or a larger N could have bitten.

## 1. The instrument (spec §10.4)

`examples/eval_probe.rs`, with its own statistics unit-tested (`cargo test -p yokohama-core --example eval_probe`, 11 tests: AUC against hand-computable truth including the mid-rank tie convention, multiplicity-equals-duplication for the cluster resample, tercile totality, the crate's nearest-rank rule, the materiality rule's three states, shape adequacy, the constant-column refusal, the proxy confusion).

Design points that are load-bearing rather than incidental:

- **Corpus.** `run_arena_game` + `GreedyAgent` factories for the greedy contestant (the honest chain: `observe` → belief sampler K=1 → `DecisionHandle` → argmax), `run_raw_game` for a `RolloutPolicy` rung (privileged by construction, labelled in the header — R1's own harness disposition, legitimate because the bot is not the subject). Per-move invariants default ON, `--no-invariants` off-switch.
- **Snapshots.** Every seat's view at every turn start, taken by REPLAYING the emitted `GameRecord` one event at a time through the same `replay_from` fold `replay`/`replay_strict` use — so the instrument cannot have its own idea of what a game did. That is checked rather than trusted, for free: a replay that diverged would reject the next move and abort the run, and 2,000 games × ~525 events folded clean.
- **One `ScoreStructure::derive` per view** (the T2-review instruction, discharged): the inner loop derives once and reads `value_of` for every (feature, player) pair. `eval::raw_value` is the convenience accessor and re-derives the whole structure per call; using it here would have paid 16 × players derivations per view instead of one.
- **Cells, not snapshots.** R1's design: a (game × seat × tercile) block aggregates to one number, and the bootstrap resamples GAMES. Consecutive turns are heavily autocorrelated and both seats of a game share its outcome, so a per-snapshot interval would be far too narrow (R1 measured per-snapshot AUCs attenuated 0.03–0.06 by the same autocorrelation).
- **Relative form**, matching the shipped evaluator: `viewer − max over opponents`. At 2P — the mandated freeze corpus — the max is over the one opponent and the orientation question `eval::opponent_extreme` answers does not arise. At 3–4P the plain max is a stated convention, recorded, not assumed away.
- **Determinism.** Everything is a function of `(master, contestant, weights, players, games, B)`. The resample stream is derived from the DESIGN and printed; nothing read off the data enters it.
- **The zero-width refusal.** A column whose every cell ties reports `constant (no signal)` rather than a printed `[0.5000, 0.5000]` — the house rule `arena_gate` §0-R3 enforces for its own estimator, applied here. It fires on real data: no seat owns a trading house in the first tercile.

**Master.** `0xE7A1_2026_0727_0001`, **diagnostic by the name of its constructor**, per 3.2 spec §3's reproduction rule. That rule reserves OS entropy for *bot-facing evaluation runs whose figures get quoted as strength claims*; nothing here is a strength claim — it is a measurement artifact whose entire value is that it re-runs — so diagnostic is the correct side of that rule, and the header prints which was used on every run.

## 4. The L14 tension, recorded and held

**The measurement runs against the shipped ordering.** L14 requires `w_core > w_on_board > w_excess` in both vectors (30 > 14 > 4 and 20 > 10 > 2). The measured separation ordering is the exact reverse at every stage:

```text
              EARLY    MID     LATE        shipped weight (early → late)
excess       0.6874  0.7200  0.6944          4 →  2     (priced LOWEST)
on_board     0.6513  0.6962  0.6759         14 → 10
core         0.5278  0.5173  0.4877         30 → 20     (priced HIGHEST)
```

**The ordering is HELD, per the task's own instruction and L14's own grounds**, and the tension is recorded here rather than resolved:

- The ordering is a **non-degeneracy condition on the one-ply argmax**, not a claim about predictive power. It is what makes a placement and a recovery *both* payable at one ply: with a single linear in-hand term, "a placement ever pays" and "a retreat sometimes pays" are the same inequality with opposite signs, and exactly one can hold. T3 measured what happens when the wrong one holds — 2P self-play placed 0 assistants and hit the turn cap.
- **An AUC ordering is not a weight ordering.** AUC measures how well a feature separates outcomes; a weight decides how much a one-unit change should move a *ranking of successors*. Pricing a near-constant highly costs a ranking almost nothing (it is nearly the same additive offset for every candidate), which is precisely why the core weight can be the largest in the table without distorting the argmax — and why the argmax it *does* distort is the one the split exists to fix, the placement/recovery boundary where in_hand crosses 2.
- Read that way the two facts are consistent rather than contradictory, and §3.4's mean column is the evidence rather than the hand-wave.
- **Routed to 3.5.** When weights are tuned rather than hand-set, this pair is the first thing tuning will pull on, and the constraint it must respect is the one-ply inequality — not the AUC ordering. Whoever does it should read L14 and this section together.

### 6.1 The §2.5 recovery floor, measured then set

Spec §2.5's falsifier: Greedy-v1's nonzero-recovery pick share over a seeded organic sample must exceed a floor set from the v1 measurement itself.

| | value |
|---|---|
| measured, frozen-table 2P corpus | **739 / 1,051,752 = 0.000703** |
| floor = half, rounded down to one significant figure (`policy_diversity`'s pattern) | **`RECOVERY_SHARE_FLOOR_2P` = 0.0003** |
| per-edit sample: the FIRST EIGHT GAMES of the same corpus (same master, same schedule, invariants off) | **3 / 4,703 = 0.000638**, 2.1× the floor |
| per-edit cost | ~3.1 s debug |

The sample is a *prefix of the published corpus* rather than an independent draw, so the test's number and this document's number are the same measurement at two sizes. Three events is thin and the test's doc says so: the corpus is the measurement, the test is the tripwire.

**Scoped to 2P, deliberately.** The share is player-count dependent and **4P sits below this floor** (35 / 135,334 = 0.000259). Widening the test without re-measuring its own floor would fail for a reason that is not a defect — the same "this is a 2P statement ONLY" discipline `PLACEMENT_CANDIDATE_CAP`'s doc carries.

**Mutation-proven** (reverted by inverse edit, never `git checkout` — L7). Re-pricing `V1`'s `assistants_in_hand_core` from `(30, 20)` to `(9, 9)` — below `assistants_on_board`, i.e. breaking the L14 ordering in the one direction that makes recovering into an empty hand a strict loss — drives the sample to zero and fails the test:

```text
§2.5 falsifier, 8 seeded 2P games (722 turns, 4738 applied moves): 0
nonzero-recovery picks, share 0.000000 against floor 0.000300
...
nonzero-recovery picks 0 of 4738 applied moves (share 0.000000) over 8 seeded 2P
games is at or below the floor 0.000300 -- spec §2.5's falsifier has gone red
```

The mutant still played eight games that ENDED (722 turns against the healthy 736), so nothing else in the suite would have noticed. That invisibility is the test's justification.

**No sweep floor moved.** The task's contingency — "re-run the sweep floors if the adjusted table moves nonzero counts" — does not fire: the table did not move, so no existing floor's measured basis changed.

**(a) The regime-mismatch residual §2.4 anticipated does not arise this time, and the reason is worth stating precisely.** §2.4 pre-registered it: after one adjustment pass the corpus describes the OLD table's play while the bot now plays the NEW one, and that mismatch is recorded rather than chased (no fixed-point iteration). Here the pass moved nothing, so the corpus regime and the shipped bot's regime are **the same regime** and the residual is null — verified, not assumed, by the byte-identical `--weights v1` re-run in §6. Had any weight moved, the residual would apply exactly as R1's critic described it one rung down.

**(b) The residual that IS live is one rung up, and T5 should read it.** These figures describe the regime **Greedy-v1** creates: 78-turn 2P games, 99.95% ChurchTrackFull endings, 27.5 candidates/decision. Lookahead-v1 (T5) prices whole turns and will inhabit a different regime — R1's own corpus-regime precondition is precisely this argument applied to the family-weighted → greedy step, and it found real movement (the warehouse magnitude nearly doubled; the in-hand signal went 0.6026 → 0.6878). **The precedent therefore says the search rung's regime should be spot-checked, not that these signs will transfer**; the instrument to do it with is now in tree, and one `eval_probe run` under a Lookahead contestant is all it costs once T5 lands a contestant to name. Not chased here: §2.4 authorizes ONE pass, and spending it against a bot that does not exist yet would be the fixed-point iteration the spec forbids.

**(c) The achievement post-claim contribution (T2 review), dispositioned as a weighting consideration.** `achievement_progress_sum` caps each leg at its own requirement, but a card ALREADY CLAIMED by the viewer stays in `view.achievements` (`AchievementCard::claimed_by` is a list, not a removal), so the term keeps paying its capped value after the claim — while the VP for that claim has already landed in `victory_points`. Part of the term's measured separation (0.5544 early → 0.6794 late, confirmed positive at both ends) is therefore re-reading VP that another feature already prices. **Disposition: it argues for keeping the weight SMALL, which it is (2 → 4, the smallest positive pair in the table, sized off the term's ~19-point scale rather than off VP).** It does NOT argue for changing the term, and §2.4's single pass moves weights, not features: filtering claimed cards out of the sum is a FEATURE-SET change and belongs to evaluator v2 / 3.5. Recorded, not acted on.

**(d) The aggregate is worse than its best member EARLY.** `EVAL TOTAL` reads 0.6414 [0.6177, 0.6659] early against `assistants_in_hand_excess`'s 0.6874 and the unsplit hand's 0.6878 — the weighted sum separates the early game *less well than one of its own terms*. Late it dominates comfortably (0.9139 against VP's 0.8571). This is the same phenomenon R1-3 recorded for the projected-score scalar (0.5806 whole against `country_bonus_now`'s 0.6212 best component) and it reinforces R1-3's shipping rule rather than contradicting it: decomposed terms, never one scalar. It is a MAGNITUDE finding — the early vector is mixing its terms in the wrong proportions — and magnitudes are outside the one-pass rule. Routed to 3.5, where the early vector is the obvious first target. (For scale only, not as a comparison: R1-2 measured a *learned* re-weighting of eval-v0 at 0.6714 early held-out on a family-weighted corpus. Different corpus, different regime, held-out vs in-sample — quoted as a rough anchor, not as a gap.)

**(e) What a confirmed sign is not.** Twenty-nine confirmed signs say each term points the right way in this regime. They say nothing about whether 12 is better than 20, and the two places where the measurement visibly disagrees with a hand-set magnitude are named above (§3.2's warehouse decay, §4's assistant triple). Magnitudes stay hand-set until 3.5.

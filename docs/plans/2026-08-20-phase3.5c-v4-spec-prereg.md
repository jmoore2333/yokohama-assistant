## 2. The value-flow ledger, operationalized (checkpoint §3's partition made checkable)

One owner per quantity along `placement → enabled action → produced asset → fulfillment →
handoff → recovery`. The table below is the ledger this spec's formulas implement; the §5
family-6/7/8 pairs are its executable form. "Retires into" names where the credit goes when
the chain advances — credit neither disappears nor persists twice.

| Quantity | Owner | Priced by | Retires into |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assistant presence (where) | S | `ProductionAccess`, `TrackAccess`, `AssistantAreasDistinct`, `BestLocalCluster` | recovery → warehouse (priced by V2's negative warehouse term) and hand-core on retrieval (V2's ordering stands, §5 family 8) |
| Enabled-action potential | S | the access features at REALIZABLE power | the action's produced asset (E-resource stocks, track presence in the mirrors) |
| Money (stock) | E-resource | `MoneyStock` | spent → fees/costs (the stock drops; nothing else re-prices the fee — F4's fix) |
| Trade goods (stock) | E-resource | `TradeGoodsStock` | consumed at fulfillment (stock drops; the order's realized value lands) |
| Realized fees | E-resource | land IN money; never a separate cumulative asset | — |
| Order potential (held) | E-order | `HeldOrderVp`, `OrderMatchedGoods`, `ExecutableOrders` | fulfillment → `VictoryPoints`, reward stocks, `CountryBonusNow`, `FlagSetMarginal` recompute (§5 family 6 pins the direction) |
| Order availability (port) | E-order | `PortMatchedGoods` (seat-matched; a shared pool is invisible to a relative form unless seat-matched) | take → held-order terms |
| Flag-set prospect | E-order | `FlagSetMarginal` (marginal-step arithmetic, §4 row 14's disposition) | realized set → `CountryBonusNow` |
| Majorities/claims "now" | incumbents | the five mirrors + `AchievementProgressSum` | terminal totals |
| Sequencing/realizability | SEARCH | never a leaf feature (§3 boundary) | — |
| Opponent's next-turn conversion | depth re-ask | out of this milestone (§2 class 3) | — |

**Within-leaf partition duties (declared so the redundancy veto has its targets):**
- `MoneyStock` (instrumental utility, always on) partitions with `AchievementProgressSum`'s
  Money10 leg (claim proximity, only when that card is live and unclaimed by the seat) and
  with the terminal-only remainder channel. **Named veto target:** whenever Money10 is in
  play and unclaimed, the achievement sum contains exactly `min(money, 10)` — algebraically
  identical to `MoneyStock` over the whole range money ≤ 10. This is the set's closest
  approach to the C1-class exact redundancy, and the step-11 instrument tests this pair
  deliberately (§11).
- `TradeGoodsStock` (generic liquidity) partitions with the goods achievement legs, the
  terminal goods-sum/3 conversion, and `OrderMatchedGoods`. The E2/O2 relationship is a
  declared ASPECT-SPLIT, bounded: O2 ≤ E2 always holds under O2's per-good capped form
  (§3.2), so O2 prices the matched FRACTION of the same physical stock E2 prices in full —
  one stock, two declared aspects, the overlap visible to the step-11 veto and the §5.5
  collinearity diagnostic.
- `HeldOrderVp` (recoverable prospect) partitions with `HandSize` (the slot-commitment
  burden, V2 weight negative — the pair prices the same cards' two different aspects and §5
  family 4(c) separates them).

### 3.2 Formula cards

Every feature is a per-seat function `v(view, player) -> i64` over PUBLIC view state,
entering evaluation through the existing relative form. Every card carries: formula, units,
sign prior (the step-9 signed-non-vacuity gate reads it), boundary analysis (the V3 lesson —
what the term does at the instant the action it encourages lands), and partition notes. NO
feature simulates a move, a route, or an action sequence (§3 contract); requirement/marginal
DISTANCE arithmetic is the sanctioned shape.

**Shared arithmetic.** `realizable_power(seat, a)` MIRRORS the engine's `calculate_power`
(game/power.rs — president +1, assistants +1 each, own shophouse +1, own trading house +1,
station +1 for all, cap 5) with exactly one substitution: the president term is an
unconditional +1 (the president joins the area when acting — and is in hand at every
hand-off state, §1 fact g, so the unconditional +1 is not an approximation there but the
literal truth of "if this seat acted at `a`") —
`min(5, own_assistants(seat, a) + 1 + own_shophouse(seat, a) + own_trading_house(seat, a) +
station_bonus(a))`, every input a public `VisiblePlayer`/view field. The audit contract pins
this helper against `calculate_power` itself on president-present states (§7). **Declared
shape:** S1/S2 apply this power at EVERY presence area simultaneously, though only one area
can be activated per turn — the features price the option portfolio, not one turn's
realization; the over-count is a declared shape, not a bug (§5.5 records its collinearity
consequence). **Declared narrowing (owner flag, §16):** S1/S2 read access only where the
seat holds ≥ 1 assistant; president-only, shophouse-only, trading-house-only, and
station-only access (all of which `calculate_power` would credit) is unpriced this slice.

**Allocation contract (binding on every card):** `score` carries a hard allocation-free pin
(`tests/eval_core.rs::score_allocates_nothing_and_explain_allocates` asserts allocs == 0).
Every helper below is therefore specified counters-and-fixed-arrays only; the seven features
needing view context (S1–S4, O1, O4, O5) ride `ScoreStructure::derive` (computed once per
score call), never `value_of`'s field-read arm. O1's pool derivation is specified
allocation-free explicitly (its card). The pin is a named member of the step-8 parity
battery (§10).

  one pass builds a fixed-size located bitset over the 36 printed order ids (completed

  keeps the commitment burden; this term prices the recoverable prospect.

  contribute 0 (declared narrowing — the F2 exhibit is about VISIBLE sitting-executable

  subset relation (§2). Units: matched goods. Sign prior: +. Boundary: FULFILLMENT retires

- **O3 `ExecutableOrders`** — count of KNOWN held orders with `req_g ≤ stock_g` for all g.

  opponent's PUBLIC (taken-order) hand. Scope honesty (checkpoint §6, verbatim duty): it

  residual is the depth re-ask's.
- **O4 `PortMatchedGoods`** — Σ over face-up port offers (both boards when `has_port_b`): Σ_g

  prior: +. A shared pool is invisible to the relative form unless seat-MATCHED — this is
  the §4 row-9 "availability" term in the only shape that can carry seat information.
  (Per-offer matching is deliberate here, unlike O2: offers are alternatives, not a held
  portfolio; the O2-style summed cap would understate a seat matched to several
  alternatives equally.)
- **O5 `FlagSetMarginal`** — max over the five countries `c` of `set_bonus(icons(seat) + 1_c)

## 5. The feasibility benchmark: pairs, margins, collateral (checkpoint §5, instantiated)

### 5.1 Conventions (fixed here, before any pair is built)

- A PAIR is two constructed legal positions (fixture-built states rendered to views;
  `Rules::standard()`; scenario-suite construction precedent) with a declared DIRECTION
  (`value(A) > value(B)` for the named seat), a declared STAGE (`p = 0` or
  `p = PROGRESS_FULL_PPM` — endpoint-exact under `interpolate`, so every pair is exactly
  linear in the integer weight vectors), and a declared MARGIN CLASS (below). **Both
  fixture sides must REALIZE the declared stage** (`progress_ppm` printed by the fixture,
  equal on both sides and to the declaration) and **both sides must be NON-TERMINAL views**
  (`terminal_standing_of == None`, asserted by the fixture — the linear form does not run
  at terminal views) — hand-off-class by default; a family whose content is intra-turn
  declares MID-TURN views explicitly in its registration (the weights are shared, so a
  mid-turn ordering constraint binds the same table; family 2(a) is the one declared
  mid-turn pair).
- **Stage-realization recipe at 2P (registered so step 2 cannot discover it late):**
  `initial_order_deck_len` at 2P is 10, so EVERY drawn order card moves the deck clock by
  100,000 ppm — a `p = 0` fixture admits NO port take and no non-full port (refill is
  automatic), capping each seat's hand at its dealt starting order; early order pairs
  therefore vary WHICH starting order a seat holds and its goods stocks, never hand size. A
  `p = PROGRESS_FULL_PPM` fixture saturates via the deck clock alone (deck empty, port
  full) OR sets the end-trigger flags consistently while staying non-terminal (not
  last-seat-TurnEnd). Per-family endpoint declarations ride §5.2; a family registered only
  at one endpoint leaves its features' other-stage weights unconstrained by the benchmark —
  recorded in the decision register (§15).
- **The pinned scale and the margin classes.** The solve pins `w_vp = (1, 40)` — V2's
  shipped VictoryPoints pair — as a hard EQUALITY constraint in both stage vectors, so
  margins are absolute integers, not functions of a free variable. Three margin classes:
  - **MARGINED:** an explicit INTEGER score threshold, computed ONCE at registration as
    `margin_score = max(1, ceil(margin_vp × w_vp(stage)))` with the pinned scale, and
    registered as that integer. A margined pair must NAME THE CARRIER — the feature or
    rules identity that justifies pricing the margined quantity in VP (e.g. the terminal
    remainder's 1 VP/2¥ and 1 VP/3-goods conversions are RULES identities; family 4(d)'s
    set-bonus step is scored VP at 1:1 in `final_total` — each such cross-feature
    calibration is declared in the pair's registration so an infeasibility can be
    attributed correctly at the minimal-contradictory-set inspection).
  - **DIRECTION-ONLY:** `margin_score = 1` (the smallest integer separation).
  - **WEAK:** `margin_score = 0` (non-strict ≥ — the checkpoint's anti-parking clause is
    weak by its own wording; only family 7 uses this class).
  The SAME registered integer thresholds are the step-9 production re-run's inequalities —
  step 4 and step 9 are literally the same constraint system, evaluated on probe columns
  and on production bytes respectively. **Consequence stated so it cannot be over-read:
  with the pinned early `w_vp` = 1, every early-endpoint MARGINED threshold floors at 1 —
  the benchmark's early half carries direction-only force; the margins' teeth are late.**
- **Collateral:** where legality forces co-movement, the fixture mechanically PRINTS its
  full 27-feature delta and the pair's registration lists the expected nonzero collateral
  rows; an unlisted nonzero row at run time is a fixture defect (stop, fix, re-freeze that
  fixture — sanctioned BEFORE covered results per §0's definition). Known forced
  collaterals registered now: goods changes move O2 (and legs when the matching achievement
  is in play — fixtures pick achievement draws that exclude the relevant legs where the
  registration says so); order fulfillment additionally produces the order's mercenary
  award and queues `PendingChoice`s for movement/warehouse/any-good faces (the "after"
  fixture declares its choice-resolution state).
- **Pair→gate mapping (fixed):** the Part-B CONSTRAINT SYSTEM is exactly the registered
  pairs of families 1–4 and 6–8. The Part-A BLOCKING SET is those same pairs PLUS the
  registered value-distinct ROOT-SUCCESSOR pairs: at DEVELOPMENT roots, registered in the
  step-2 package (development ordinals are readable at step 2); at ESCROW roots,
  RECIPE-INSTANTIATED only at the step-10 read (§6 — the recipe is frozen in the step-2
  package, the roots stay unread). Family 5 is a formula property tested in §7's audit
  contract and enters neither gate. Family 9 (positive controls) runs at steps 5/9 only.
  Family 10 is recorded-only diagnostics (§4). Organic collisions outside the registered
  and recipe-instantiated pairs are report-only, never blocking.
- Every pair is registered as `(id, family, seat, stage, direction, margin class + integer,
  carrier + declared calibration where margined, collateral list, construction recipe)`;
  seat = side A's mover unless the registration states otherwise.
  **Registered pair counts (exact, replacing minimums):** family 1: 4 · family 2: 2 ·

### 5.3 Part A — the collision test (weight-free)

**Signature (fixed):** the tuple `(exact progress_ppm, Δv over the 27 features)` where Δv is

### 5.4 Part B — the bounded compatibility solve

margin_score(pair)` at its declared endpoint stage (54 integer variables minus the two

**Procedure (two halves, deterministic, in-repo, probe/example space):**
1. **Witness search:** define TOTAL VIOLATION as

   early-then-late); at each coordinate try ALL steps {±16, ±8, ±4, ±2, ±1} and take the

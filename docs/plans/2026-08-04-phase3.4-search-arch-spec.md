**0.1 The ADR is the gate artifact, and its decision rule is pre-registered.** The ADR is a dated document (`docs/plans/2026-08-XX-phase3.4-architecture-decision.md`, the depth-decision artifact's shape: pre-registration quoted verbatim → method → verbatim cell outputs → realized Var_pair + post-hoc detectable edge → THE DECISION applied mechanically → what the decision does NOT say → the evidence that would reverse it → close-out drafts). Before ANY registered cell runs, a **pre-registration addendum is committed** carrying: the selected MCTS-v1 knobs (chosen by §4's bounded screening protocol, itself pre-registered here), every adjudication master (drawn by serial order in a fresh family, T7's convention), the cell table with N per cell, and the decision rule below — L19's discipline: total rules written before the run, masters by serial order, pilots disclosed.

**The decision rule (TOTAL, with the critic's R1-2/R1-3/R1-4 repairs built in).** The decision is taken on the PRIMARY cell (§5's C2: MCTS-v1 vs capped-S1 at the headline budget, node-matched, N = 200 pair units), **gated on its same-master null control** (§5's C1) and **declared only when BOTH primary arms agree** (§0.2). Branches, total over `IntervalOutcome` and the non-completion surfaces:
1. Interval entirely above 0.5 (or all-won refusal, bound 1−3/200 = 0.985 > 0.5) in both arms → **MCTS-v1 adopted** as the serious-strength architecture for 3.5.
2. Interval entirely below 0.5 (or all-lost refusal) in both arms → **budget-scaled S1 retained**; MCTS-v1 preserved as a labelled diagnostic (nothing is deleted — the T7 disposition).
3. Interval containing 0.5, or a constant-interior refusal (at 2P that is exactly θ̂ = 0.5 — R1's observation that T7's "other constant interior → bug" branch is 2P-vacuous), in either arm, or the two arms disagreeing in direction → **no edge at the realized power (reported, with the detectable-edge bound the cells actually had)** → the tie-breaker is the WALL axis at matched node-budget (§0.3's second axis): if the wall-matched qualifier (§5's C3) shows a direction, the cheaper-per-wallclock arm is named the working architecture WITH the no-node-edge finding recorded; if it too contains the null, **the incumbent (S1) is retained on maturity grounds** and the ADR records "no edge detected at matched budget at this power".
4. Null-control suspension: if C1's interval excludes 0.5, the primary's adjudication is SUSPENDED and the primary + null re-run ONCE at the SECOND pre-declared master (named in the addendum, not chosen after the failure); two consecutive null failures = a harness-defect ticket, and the ADR records "the instrument could not resolve a zero edge on two masters" as the finding (R1-3 repair).
5. Non-completion, three distinct surfaces, none silently scored (R1-7): a game hitting `HONEST_MAX_TURNS` (`ArenaError::CapReached`) INVALIDATES the cell — the cell is reported as `completion-failure` for that (arm, budget) and the adjudication falls to the next lower ladder budget, with the ADR stating the headline comparison could not be made (R1-4 repair); search-internal ply-cap hits are a labelled measurement caveat (the cell stands, the caveat prints — the 3.3 x_ply_cap_hits lesson); budget starvation (`budget_starved_decisions` > 0 where the design says 0) is an arm-underpowered label, reported per cell.
6. Any other outcome shape → harness bug; investigate; no adjudication.

**The wall-matched qualifier** (R1-2 repair): C3's outcome enters every branch as a named four-value qualifier — `agrees-above` / `agrees-below` / `contains-null` / `disagrees-in-direction` — printed in the ADR beside the primary's branch, never silently absorbed.

**0.2 Two arms, T7-extended.** The primary cell runs TWICE: arm 1 on the committed diagnostic master (the regression-able artifact), arm 2 on an OS-entropy master drawn at run time and recorded. Rationale: T7 ran one committed master and its §8 conceded a fresh-master re-run is "the cheap way to buy" robustness; at MATCHED budgets θ̂ may sit near 0.5, exactly where a master effect could flip a direction, so agreement of the two arms is part of the rule (branch 3 handles disagreement as no-edge — a disagreement IS the finding that the edge is not robust at this N). This is a design adjudication, not a promotion: no roster test asserts a direction (the repo's ONE direction-asserting gate cell remains 3.3's), and ledger row 19's two-arm shape is borrowed for robustness, not for a strength claim.

**0.3 "Report strength-per-node and strength-per-wallclock" (map verbatim; they can rank differently), operationalized.** The budget currency is **scored successors per decision** — one scored successor = one apply+observe+eval of a candidate (1.359 µs; the cross-architecture unit both arms literally pay, and ≈88% of S1's wall) — imposed as a **hard deterministic per-decision cap on post-root work** (§3.4 defines the meter and the root exemption; both arms share the same root enumeration+scoring by construction, so root cost cancels and is reported). The ADR carries the two-axis table: per (arm × budget) — θ̂ vs the common anchor, realized post-root scored/dec, realized total scored/dec, realized in-cell ms/dec FOR BOTH SIDES (R3-1's repair: no cell is ever labelled "matched" on an out-of-cell figure) — and states the per-node ranking and the per-wallclock ranking side by side. **Regime conditionality is stated wherever a cost figure appears** (the L18 classic, measured this cycle at ×2.13: S1 spends 770 scored/dec at its own states and 1,639 at greedy-play states — no cost constant travels without its state-regime qualifier).

**0.4 Candidate (c) — information-set variants — does NOT enter the experiment, and the grounds are pre-committed.** The map admits (c) "only if determinization's pathologies (strategy fusion, non-locality) are *measured* to matter here". The measurements: the evidence-selected MCTS design carries an evaluator leaf (playouts priced out ~500× at 2P — two streams, two arithmetics); the eval-leaf prototype's cross-world choice divergence read 7.5% on an instrument whose ZERO is validated (greedy/lookahead 0/80) but whose SCALE is not (no positive control; part may be tree-shape dice divergence — critic R3-2); and the repo's own precedent says divergence is not value (B=4 diverges from B=1 at 7.25% and wins 0.75). "Measured to matter" is therefore NOT established, and (c) stays out of 3.4. What the ADR records as (c)'s entry evidence for the above-bar band: the divergence figures with their instrument caveat, and the named follow-up instrument (a positive control with known analytic world-sensitivity, or a pre-registered ORDERING rather than a level). The in-repo leakage extension (§7) asserts leakage-invariance for MCTS-v1 and RECORDS world-sensitivity — the two are different quantities and the module doc says so.

**0.5 Every strength figure of this phase is JOINT (architecture × `WeightTable::V1`), and V1 is FROZEN for the whole experiment.** No weight is touched anywhere in 3.4; a weight change is a per-controller version bump that stops figure comparability (3.3 §0.5 / L12), and the reversal-evidence section of the ADR names "evaluator v2" as a condition of the finding exactly as the depth decision did.

## §2 — Design inputs the module is built against (measured, cited to the research notes)

1. **Evaluator leaf, hand-off valuation.** ~40% of a naive per-move UCT's leaf values are read MID-TURN — states V1 was never calibrated on and which S1 by construction never evaluates; the turn-boundary insight (T7) re-arrives in the MCTS context as a leaf rule. MCTS-v1's default leaf value is therefore taken at a HAND-OFF state: an unexpanded node reached mid-turn is valued by completing the turn greedily (beam-1, the S1 continuation machinery's semantics) and evaluating V1 at the hand-off; a raw mid-turn eval remains as a screening-diagnostic knob (§4) so the mechanism's contribution is measured, not assumed.
2. **Expansion is the cost driver** (56.8% of a naive sim; UCT pays a full enumeration per scored successor vs S1's 4.46 amortization) and **Placement roots are 94% of S1's node budget from 15.5% of points** — so the allocation design (what gets scored, in what order, under the cap) matters more than any tree constant.
3. **Proposal/ordering composite** (R4): eval-order the materialized candidate list at the root and at nodes with >16 candidates (~15% of nodes; ordering every node exhausts the budget); retreat admission by keyed distinct INDEX over `retreat_len` (never by materialized move — measured duplicate rate 10→9.04); placement widening operates over the node's materialized Vec, never the family (`get(i)` is O(i) — R4's friction list). Progressive widening BEYOND the shipped candidate scheme's own caps is an allocation question left to §4's screening (coverage does not need it at 2P; allocation evidence points toward ordering + UCB doing the work).
4. **Value normalization is explicit** (R5's measured foot-gun: raw-scale UCB makes c inert): leaf values map to [0,1] by a declared monotone squash of the V1 relative margin (the mapping and its scale constant are named in the module and in provenance; the scale is a screening knob, disclosed).
5. **L10 discipline**: a node owns `(GameState, Vec<Move>, stats)`; `DecisionHandle` is a scoped temporary at expansion — ONE enumeration per node, pinned harness-scoped; the forced fast path is cached (59.9% of decision points are forced; 84.6% of beam-tree nodes — a proxy figure, labelled).
6. **Tie-breaks**: uniform keyed draw at every selecting point (root argmax over visit counts, UCB selection ties, expansion pick), constant-consumption one-u64 multiply-shift (ledger row 18); the degeneracy pin applies (a constant-valued leaf must still place and terminate).
7. **Keyed streams**: the agent inherits `agent_stream_seed`'s `(world_seed, seat, slot)` keying; per-decision and per-node streams derive by counter keying, never by consumption order that tree shape can perturb (the CRN discipline that makes cells byte-identical at a master).
8. **Memory is an ops fact, not a cut line** (map §1.3): ~3.4 KB/node, ~0.3M nodes/GB, matched-budget trees ~MBs. No memory-driven design constraint exists at 2P.

## §3 — The MCTS-v1 contestant (`game/mcts.rs`)

**3.1 Honest chain, verbatim:** `observe(state, seat)` → `BeliefSampler` K=1 → ONE sampled world → search over that world → chosen `Move` validated by `is_move_legal` at the driver boundary exactly as every contestant. Sampler failure is a typed hard error with no fallback arm. K is a knob that exists and defaults to 1; K>1 is out of scope (§0.4) and the knob's doc says so.

**3.2 The tree.** Per DECISION (fresh each decision, no reuse): root = the current decision point in the sampled world; edges = single `Move`s (the §3.3 candidate scheme); descent by UCB1 over normalized values (`c` a knob, default from §4 screening); unexpanded node → expand (one `DecisionHandle` enumeration, materialize candidates, §2.3 ordering where it applies) → leaf value by §2.1's hand-off completion + V1 → backprop from the ROOT SEAT's perspective (opponent-decision nodes select by the acting seat's value — 1 − v at 2P; the convention is stated in-module). Terminal states value by `final_result` win/loss for the root seat (the clockwise tie-break inside `final_result`, never re-implemented). The root decision = keyed-uniform draw over the max-visit set (tie rule §2.6).

**3.3 Candidates:** exactly the shipped scheme (routes whole from the handle memo; retreat = zero-recovery anchor + all-recover anchor + M samples, M=8 default; placement = realized-count probe against the 512 cap with the null-placement anchor; other families whole) — no new candidate semantics, so the factored-action equivalence surface is untouched.

**3.4 The budget meter and cap (the matched-budget instrument, shared).** A `ScoredBudget` meter counts candidate evaluations (apply+observe+eval units). The ROOT enumeration+scoring is exempt-but-metered (both arms pay the identical root by construction — same scheme, same evaluator — so it cancels; realized root spend is reported per cell). The cap binds on POST-ROOT work: MCTS-v1 checks before starting each simulation and stops when the next would exceed the cap (a started simulation completes; realized spend and bind rate are reported); `lookahead-v1` gains an optional `cap=<n>` knob with deterministic truncation semantics (beam continuation halts when the cap is reached; the best completed hand-off evaluation stands; a decision whose cap bound before ANY continuation completed increments `budget_starved_decisions`). **Cap OFF is the default and the shipped `lookahead-v1:b=4` path is byte-identical to today — pinned by the §6 goldens.** Both contestants surface the same counters: realized post-root scored/dec, cap-bind count, `budget_starved_decisions`.

**3.5 Explanations (the bar's third ingredient does not pause for architecture work — map §3).** MCTS-v1's explanation is the ROOT TABLE: per root candidate — visits, mean normalized value, the V1 hand-off evaluation of its first completion, and the chosen move's margin over the runner-up; rendered through the same `ExplainHandle` shape (a new `Mcts` tally arm), with `x_explanations == Some(x_applied_moves)` riding every cell it plays. CLI `suggest` integration is OUT (3.5 decides which architecture suggest speaks for); the surface exists and is tested at module level.

**3.6 Provenance:** controller name `mcts-v1`, its own `MCTS_IMPL_VERSION` const (§0.5/L12 per-controller rule), params carrying every knob (cap, c-ppm, squash scale, m, k, leaf mode) plus `weights=<identity>`; runner grammar `mcts-v1[:cap=<n>,c=<ppm>,m=<n>,...]` with unknown knobs as errors.

## §4 — The bounded screening protocol (pre-registered here; multiplicity controlled)

MCTS has free variables S1 never had (c, squash scale, leaf mode). The tuning budget is BOUNDED and DECLARED: a screening grid of at most **12 configurations** — leaf mode {hand-off, mid-turn-diag} × c ∈ {3 declared values} × squash scale ∈ {2 declared values} — each at N=30 pair units vs `lookahead-v1:b=4,cap=B*` on SCREENING masters (a declared family disjoint from the adjudication family), selection = best θ̂ with ties to the simpler config. The screening table and the selected config are committed in the pre-registration addendum BEFORE any registered cell runs; the registered cells run the selected config and nothing else; no registered master is observed during screening. A screening sweep is a disclosed pilot, not evidence — no screening figure is quoted as a strength claim. (This is the L19 shape: the SELECTION PROCEDURE is pre-registered, so the selection cannot be post-hoc; the 3.3 precedent is b=4 chosen from research before its registered run.)

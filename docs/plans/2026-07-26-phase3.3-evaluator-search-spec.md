## §0 — The gate, made executable

Map §5-3.3's gate: *"beats family-weighted random with honest intervals (paired, seat-rotated); scenario suite v1 green; every chosen move explainable; leakage invariants green against the new bot. Preserved as a rung."* Unlike 3.2's gate, no clause here is unsatisfiable as written — the arena, the interval machinery, and the leakage harness all exist. What §0 does is pre-commit the executable reading of each clause so a green run cannot be vacuous, and record the one deliberate contrast with `arena_gate`'s no-direction rule. Nothing below restates the map's gate; if the clean-eyes review finds any reading substantive enough to count as a restatement, it lands three-site (spec §0, map §11 A3, guidingpath row) per the A2 protocol.

**0.1 "Beats family-weighted random with honest intervals" — the promotion cell.** Contestant under test: **Lookahead-v1** (§4's search rung — the 3.3 bot) as X vs `RolloutPolicy::FAMILY_WEIGHTED_DEFAULT` as Y. 2P; unit = seed-paired seat-swapped pair; **N = 200 units / 400 games** — the 3.2 spec §4 promotion-cell doctrine (MME 0.60 → 200 pairs from measured Var_pair), reiterated by the owner's 3.3 kickoff; NOT a figure from the map, which states no N (research-notes §5 sizing note). Primary interval: percentile bootstrap over unit shares, B = 10,000. **"Beats" =** the bootstrap 95% interval sits entirely above 0.5, **or** the cell produces the all-won zero-width refusal, whose rule-of-three one-sided 95% lower bound (1 − 3/200 = 0.985) is above 0.5 — pre-committed here because the research floor (greedy alone at θ̂ 0.9292) makes a blowout the likely outcome, and a gate that only accepts a two-sided interval would fail exactly when the bot is strongest. The gate cell runs on a committed **diagnostic master, single-threaded** (3.2 §3's gate-cell exemption: an OS-entropy gate flakes at the nominal rate by construction and the byte-identical-reproduction pin would be unstatable); the gate figure is therefore a fact about that master's 200 units and is stated as such. Beside it, **recorded never asserted**: one OS-entropy run of the same cell — the figure any strength CLAIM quotes (3.2 §3's rule), plus the two lattice cells of §5.4 (greedy-v1 vs FW; Lookahead-v1 vs greedy-v1).
**Direction IS asserted here, deliberately** — the one contrast with `arena_gate`, whose cells validate the INSTRUMENT and therefore print directions rather than assert them. This gate's subject is the BOT; the instrument's own validation (null cell, synthetic coverage, refusal path, CRN keying) stands in `arena_gate` and is not re-litigated by this cell.
**The miss path, pre-committed:** if the promotion cell misses, 3.3 does NOT close by softening the reading (no "interval overlaps 0.5", no N change, no master shopping — ledger row 16's no-peeking rule applies to re-runs). The bot iterates — weights within §2's hand-set discipline, search knobs within §4 — and the cell re-runs as a NEW pre-declared attempt, each attempt recorded. L5: fix the bot, not the claim.

**0.2 "Scenario suite v1 green"** — `tests/scenario_suite.rs`, per-edit tier, never `#[ignore]`d. Every scenario: a constructed state (via `place_setup_dummies` and state builders), a seat, a falsifiable property of the bot's decision or the evaluator's ordering, and a rules citation. **Authoring anti-vacuity rule:** a scenario is admitted only with a demonstrated failing counterpart — it must FAIL against a named deliberately-broken evaluator variant (recorded in the test's doc comment) so a scenario that can't fail can't pass vacuously. Never-delete rule per the map's threads. Count is plan-set (floor: the eight §6 categories each represented).

**0.3 "Every chosen move explainable"** — two executable halves. (a) Per-edit property: `explain(view)`'s term contributions SUM EXACTLY to `score(view)` — integer arithmetic, no tolerance — over organically-sampled views at 2/3/4P. (b) The promotion cell's driver asserts an `Explanation` was produced for EVERY move the evaluator bot played (count == the bot's applied moves; there is no unexplained-move arm to fall back to). CLI `suggest my move` renders the same explanation (§7); guide pair updated in the same commit.

**0.4 "Leakage invariants green against the new bot"** — the evaluator agent joins `tests/observation_leakage.rs`'s `run_decision_invariance_sweep` as a new cell beside the canary and the FamilyWeighted cell (the sweep was extracted in 3.2 for exactly this), with the agent re-seeded identically per comparison as the FamilyWeighted cell does; plus an evaluator analog of `family_weighted_weight_vector_is_invariant_under_hidden_variation`: `score()` and `explain()` are INVARIANT under hidden variation of the true state (the variants observe back to the same view). Sampler failure stays a typed hard error with no fallback arm — inherited from the `ArenaAgent` seam, restated because the leakage suite is where a silent fallback would surface as a "flaky" invariance failure.

**0.5 "Preserved as a rung"** — both new bots become NAMED, versioned, reproducible arena contestants (§5): descriptor-driven provenance (no hand-typed names), `from_str`-reachable in `examples/arena`, corpus-filable, and re-runnable from a record's provenance alone. Version discipline: the evaluator carries its own **`EVAL_IMPL_VERSION`**, distinct from `POLICY_IMPL_VERSION` — bumping the policy const re-versions every policy rung's corpora (the 3.2 carry), and the evaluator must be re-versionable without touching them.

## §2 — Evaluator v1 (`game/eval.rs`)

**2.1 Type-level honesty (L12).** Input is `&PlayerView` exclusively. The module imports no `GameState`, no `PendingSetup`, and nothing from `game::` that could carry one — a feature reading hidden state must not compile (R1's compiler-enforced pattern, adopted for the shipped module). Scores are `i64` integer arithmetic end-to-end: deterministic, Eq-safe, and what makes §0.3's exact explanation sum possible. No floats inside the evaluator (floats live in stats only).

**2.2 Feature set.** R1's redundancy-pruned relative set, one member per §1-R1-10 cluster, every feature computed for the viewer AND per-opponent from `view.players`' public fields, combined in RELATIVE form (viewer − best opponent at 2P; viewer − max over opponents at 3–4P — interfaces N-generic per map §1.2; research-notes §4.5: ship relative). The set: `victory_points`; **`country_bonus_now`** (the end-game country-set bonus re-derived from the view — the best measured early separator, AUC 0.6212); `completed_orders`; `technologies`; `church_count`; `customs_count`; `assistants_in_hand`; **`assistants_in_warehouse`** (NEGATIVE — the largest fitted magnitude, and one half of the convergent recovery-pricing defect); `assistants_on_board`; `shophouses_built`; `trading_houses_built`; `five_power_tokens`; `imported_goods`; `achievement_progress_sum`; `mercenaries_total`; `hand_size` (NEGATIVE). Score-structure terms (country/church/customs/tech bonus arithmetic) are re-derived from `game/scoring.rs`'s breakdowns **as view-side functions, shipped DECOMPOSED with per-term weights** — never folded into one projected-score scalar (one component out-separates the aggregate, research-notes R1-3). **Excluded with recorded grounds** (research-notes R1-7/R1-8): money, goods totals, hand-order proximity (inverted late), and every linear end-trigger global (AUC exactly 0.5 by construction — within-game constants cannot separate seats).

**2.3 Stage shape: smooth interpolation, not a switch.** Weights are TWO hand-set endpoint vectors (`W_EARLY`, `W_LATE`) interpolated by a continuous, view-measurable **progress proxy** `p ∈ [0, 1]`: the maximum over the end-trigger clocks' proximity fractions derivable from the view (order-deck depletion vs its player-count initial size; church-track occupancy; customs-track occupancy; per-player shophouse and trading-house completion) — rules-structural (the game ends at whichever clock fires first), transparent, and built from the observables the critic measured at r ≈ −0.75 against true stage. A hard tercile switch is rejected on the measured ~35% mis-assignment floor (research-notes §1, critic correction). `explain()` reports each term's INTERPOLATED weight beside its raw value, so the stage shape is visible in every explanation. Signs that differ by stage carry R1's measured directions (church early-negative under weak play) **subject to 2.4 before freezing**.

**2.4 The weight-freeze precondition (research-notes §1, promoted by the critic).** R1's corpus is family-weighted self-play; the shipped greedy rung plays materially differently (78.9–80.8 turns, ~86% ChurchTrackFull). Before `W_EARLY`/`W_LATE` are frozen: regenerate the corpus **under Greedy-v1 self-play** via `examples/eval_probe` (§10.4) and re-measure the three named figures — early VP AUC, the `church_count` early sign, the `assistants_in_warehouse` magnitude. A materially-moved sign moves the weight; each check and its disposition is recorded in the execution ledger. The probe also records the §2.3 proxy's accuracy against post-hoc stage (validation, recorded not gated).

**2.5 Recovery pricing (the convergent defect, fixed by construction).** eval-v0's on_board(18)/in_hand(8) made every nonzero-recovery retreat a strict loss — argmax 0 of 48,458 movement picks, the RetreatAverse pathology reproduced from the evaluator side. v1's warehouse/in-hand/on-board triple prices assistant POSITION explicitly. **Falsifier, per-edit:** under v1 weights, Greedy-v1's nonzero-recovery pick share over a seeded organic sample is **> 0** (exact floor measured-then-set at plan time from the v1 measurement itself, per the house rule) — the evaluator-side analog of `policy_diversity`'s sampler-side gate. Consequence for §3.3: the retreat-candidate knob M is NOT settled by R2's M-is-a-no-op finding, which was eval-v0-conditional.

**2.6 Terminal states.** A terminal successor (game over in lookahead) is scored from the view's own final ordering facts (VP totals visible in-view) via a dedicated terminal term large enough to dominate any positional sum, with the clockwise tie-break respected. Constant and shape stated in code; exercised by a scenario (§6) rather than trusted (R2: 607 terminal scorings in 5M — organically rare).

## §3 — The greedy rung (`Greedy-v1`)

**3.1 Honest chain.** `GreedyAgent` implements `ArenaAgent` with `PolicyAgent`'s chain verbatim: own ChaCha8 stream → sampler seed → `BeliefSampler::from_view` → one world → `DecisionHandle::new(world)` → §3.3 candidates → §2 scores on `apply_move_unchecked` successors via `observe(successor, seat)` → argmax under §3.2 → `DecisionReport` carrying the volume from the SAME handle. Setup Keep decision per §8. Sampler refusal: typed hard error, no fallback.

**3.2 The tie-break rule (spec-level, the research package's headline).** Argmax ties resolve by a **uniform draw over the tied set from the agent's own RNG stream**. Grounds: worth 0.59–0.67 in θ̂ across two independent harnesses (0.9292 vs 0.2633 same-everything-else), because the engine's Placement family enumerates the null placement first and evaluators tie on ~30% of decisions — first-listed-wins turns enumeration order into policy. **Pin (per-edit):** a constant-valued evaluator over the greedy agent must NOT produce the degenerate all-empty-placement game (assert its games place assistants and terminate without cap; the R2 probe made executable). Consequences accepted: the rungs are stochastic-per-stream (provenance already carries `agent_seed`); determinism holds per (master, unit, seat, slot) keying, preserving §0.1's byte-identical gate-cell pin.

**3.3 Candidate scheme (bounded; the exploding families never materialize).** Routes: ALL labels from the handle memo (max observed in one candidate set: 17, 2P organic greedy — an observation, not a ceiling; L10 preserved: one enumeration per decision point). Retreat: never materialized — the zero-recovery anchor, the all-recover anchor, plus **M = 8** `retreat_sample` draws; M stays a knob because its measured no-op was eval-v0-conditional (§2.5), and the v1 pick-share falsifier is what validates it. Placement: iterate all realized combos when ≤ 512 (2P-safe: cap bound 0 of 48,458, max realized 396), else **the sampling fallback — which is KNOWN to be the live arm at 4P** (the repo's own `action_cost` fixture realizes 3,231 combos, 6.3× the cap) **and has never executed in any research measurement**: it gets dedicated coverage (a 4P fixture cell exercising the fallback with a distribution sanity check) before the scheme is called settled. All other families: full enumeration. Deterministic per stream; max candidate set observed 396 (2P organic).

**3.4 Cost envelope (research figures, re-measured in-repo at plan time):** greedy decision ≈ 21 µs (isolate-composed 21.3, whole-scheme 21.4 — 6% cross-harness agreement); `observe` 0.609 µs = 45% of a candidate's 1.359 µs, so anything that re-observes per node is observe-bound — the fact §4.4's cache design is built on.

## §4 — The shallow-search rung (`Lookahead-v1`) and the depth-semantics decision

**4.1 The decision protocol (map open decision #2, settled IN 3.3 by a pre-registered experiment).** Research's corrected evidence points to **turn-boundary** (atomic-with-quiescence lost the head-to-head 0.1667 [0.0667, 0.2833] on completed games at ~equal corrected cost — handle ratio 1.08:1), but the decisive rows are 30-unit screening cells and the beam's internal stable-sort tie-break was never randomized — so the map's "settled here empirically" is discharged by an experiment 3.3 RUNS, not by adopting screening rows. **Pre-registered design:** S2(d=2) vs S1(B=4), both schemes' EVERY tie-point randomized (root argmax, greedy continuation steps, beam-frontier truncation — a beam frontier tie resolves by uniform draw among tied tails), evaluator v1, K=1, 2P, N = 200 units (the MME-0.60 doctrine; ledger row 16 satisfied), completed games only (with the §3.2 rule in place research observed 0 cap hits in ~1,900 games; a cap hit in the experiment is investigated as a bug, not adjudicated), committed diagnostic master, run as an `examples/arena` cell over §5's contestant grammar. **Decision rule, pre-committed:** interval entirely below 0.5 → turn-boundary confirmed (S2 is X); entirely above → atomic adopted instead and this section's rung definition flips; interval containing 0.5 → the cheaper scheme at measured equal strength wins (deterministic-count cost, not wall). The outcome lands as a dated artifact (`docs/plans/…-phase3.3-depth-decision.md`), a guidingpath decision-log row, and the map §8 row-2 discharge. Both schemes ship behind the contestant surface either way — the loser stays available as a labelled diagnostic contestant (it is 3.4's raw material), only the winner is the preserved rung.

**4.2 The rung (assuming turn-boundary per current evidence; §4.1 governs).** `Lookahead-v1` = S1: choose the current move by completing THE REST OF OWN TURN after each root candidate (hand-off = `current_player` transition; game end counts as hand-off), beam width **B = 4** over the turn's move sequence, evaluating §2's v1 at the hand-off; root candidates and within-turn steps use §3.3's candidate scheme and §3.2's tie-break. B stays a knob (B=1 measured 0.86 vs greedy; B=4 beats B=1 at 0.75 head-to-head — screening figures, directions only).

**4.3 K worlds: K = 1, with the revisit trigger recorded.** Root determinization at K>1 measured ZERO divergence (0/13,220 aggregate-vs-world-0; bit-identical games in two harnesses) at K× cost, for a structural reason: no v1 term reads a field the sampler determinizes. **Trigger, recorded here and in the module doc:** K>1 becomes live the moment an evaluator term (or search-visible quantity) reads the order deck, tech deck, boxed pile, or an opponent's hidden starting order; the acceptance test for any future K knob is **divergence rate** (cheap, deterministic), never a win-rate cell — unmeasurable by construction at zero divergence. One stated caveat: S1's turn completion DOES apply moves that draw from the determinized deck (port refills during lookahead), so the trigger includes "an evaluator term that reads port/lab offers or any drawn-card-dependent quantity" — v1 reads none.

**4.4 Cost + cache design (ledger L10's row-11 shape, delivered).** Search cost is observe-bound (§3.4). Budget: ~1.0–1.1 ms/decision at B=4 (the CORRECTED figure — the published 700 µs was measured under first-wins play whose impoverished states made turn completions artificially cheap; research-notes §3). A search node owns `(GameState, Vec<Move> candidates, i64 eval)`; the `DecisionHandle` is a **scoped temporary per node** (it borrows `&GameState` and cannot be owned by the node) — built AT MOST ONCE per node, which is the L10 discipline generalized from "per decision point" to "per (node × world)". The **forced-fast-path**: ≈60% of decision points offer ≤1 candidate, and discovering that costs a full enumeration — the node cache stores the forced answer so a revisit never re-enumerates (the duplicate-handle defect the critic found in R3's S2 is the named hazard). No per-world trees (§4.3). **Counter-pin extension (the kickoff's named deliverable):** the L10 cfg-gated DFS counter's harness assertion (==1 per on-board Movement decision point) gains a search-scoped form — expected count = nodes-with-on-board-Movement expanded × K, asserted in the search harness's tests; the existing policy-harness pin is untouched. Structural sizing constants (forced share, per-phase branching, hand-off length p99 ≤ 12 / max observed 14) are RE-MEASURED under the shipped tie-break at plan time before any is written into code as a bound (research-notes §3: the shape holds, the constants move).

## §5 — Arena integration: the contestant seam

**5.1 The widening.** `CellSpec` is `RolloutPolicy`-keyed and cannot host an evaluator bot; `run_arena_game` already takes agent factories. `game/arena/stats.rs` gains a **`Contestant`** enum — `Policy(RolloutPolicy)` plus the evaluator-bot arm(s) carrying their knobs (search shape, B, K, M, `EVAL_IMPL_VERSION`) — with `CellSpec` re-keyed over `Contestant` (`Policy` arms behave byte-identically to today: pinned by re-running an existing gate cell's tabulation unchanged). Factory stays INSIDE the driver; agents built from `(seat, player_count)` only.

**5.2 Provenance discipline.** `ControllerProvenance` for evaluator contestants comes from a descriptor on the contestant (name, `EVAL_IMPL_VERSION`, knob map in declared integer units) — never hand-typed at a call site, mirroring `RolloutPolicy::descriptor`. CRN keying preserved: play streams `(world_seed, seat, contestant-slot)`, setup streams `(world_seed, seat)` — the null-cell standing self-test must hold with evaluator contestants, exercised by §10.2's smoke.

**5.3 Runner grammar.** `examples/arena cell` grows contestant parsing (e.g. `greedy-v1[:m=8]`, `lookahead-v1[:b=4,k=1]`, the diagnostic atomic arm) so the §0.1 gate cell, the §4.1 experiment, and any future rematch are committed COMMANDS, not bespoke harnesses.

**5.4 The rung lattice at close (recorded beside the gate):** Greedy-v1 vs FW; Lookahead-v1 vs Greedy-v1. ## §7 — Explanations + CLI `suggest my move`

`Explanation`: ordered term contributions (feature id, raw value, interpolated weight, product) summing exactly to the score; for search-chosen moves additionally the principal continuation (the completed own-turn sequence to the evaluated hand-off) and the top-k runner-up moves with score margins. Explanation text states term contributions factually (a predictor's terms, not play advice — R1's correlation-not-causation caveat carried into wording). CLI: `suggest` in sim mode for a human seat — runs the evaluator agent's `choose` on THE HUMAN SEAT'S OWN VIEW (honest by construction; bot-hand masking and the privacy toggle unchanged), renders move + explanation + margins; deliberately absent in live mode (3.2's CLI scope stands). Guide pair updated in the same commit; `guide_sync` enforces the mechanical slice. Explanations for the Keep decision per §8.

## Lessons carried (L1–L15 stand in the spec ledger and guidingpath; NEW from the 3.3 cycle, for the next orchestrator)

- **L16 — subagent-failure recovery.** A 529-dead implementer returns `null` into the workflow script: null-guard every `impl.status` read; on repeated 529s BACK OFF (15 → 30 min timers) rather than hammering; when a dead implementer leaves uncommitted work, dispatch a CONTINUATION implementer to *assess-then-complete* it (assess first with `git diff`; never discard wholesale) — the T10 recovery preserved ~34 minutes of sound work.
- **L17 — exit codes, unpiped.** `cargo clippy … | tail` reports the PIPE's status; a red clippy sailed through once. Check `$?` on the unpiped command; the rule went into every subsequent brief.
- **L18 — re-derive research figures for the SHIPPED bot before quoting them at gate time.** Three separate 3.3 incidents, one pattern: R3's ladder contaminated by an undocumented tie-break; the promotion gate's "≈8×10⁻¹⁴ refusal probability" computed at a weaker probe's 0.86 sweep rate (the refusal FIRED — correctly pre-committed, but the expectation was for a different bot); a detectable-edge computed at the θ̂-realized variance instead of the alternative's. Every research probability, power figure, or structural constant is conditional on the harness that measured it.
- **L19 — pre-registration with TOTAL decision rules works.** T7 adjudicated mechanically with zero re-litigation because the rule was written total over `IntervalOutcome` before the run, the master chosen by serial order before any cell, and the pilot disclosed. 3.4's entire purpose is such an experiment — write the ADR's decision rule the same way, and design for the refusal arms (see the field problem).
- **L20 — quarantine conditional findings with falsifiers; mutation-test which test is the detector.** The M-no-op was eval-v0-conditional and would have hardened into the design; the divergence-zero assertion turned out to be the SOLE detector of a keying regression (both named pins stayed green under both canonical mutations) — before asserting any measured zero, find out which test actually detects the regression it guards.
- **Also validated end-to-end:** the goldens-before-reseam pattern (T0's projection-contract goldens caught the exact slot-keying mutation at T6 and tolerated the sanctioned additive delta by design) — repeat it for any 3.4 seam change; and the sweep-rate honesty note — record which interval SHAPE each gate produced (two-sided vs one-sided bound), since the two are different quantitative statements.
- **Sizing note:** 3.3 ran ~24 h wall / roughly 9–10 M subagent tokens across the research workflow (4 agents), spec review (2), 13 task pairs, 4 orchestrator fix-wave agents, and the six-lens review (7). Budget 3.4 similarly if its scope is comparable; the discriminating experiment itself is compute-heavy (T7's single cell was 12 min release — an MCTS arm at matched budget will cost more).

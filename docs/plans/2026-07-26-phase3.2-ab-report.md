## 1. The A/B at arena scale

**Command:** `cargo run --release -p yokohama-core --example arena -- ab` (defaults: 400 2P pairs, 100 4P rotation blocks, 200 drift-watch games). Total wall **25.31s**.

### Headline

**The E2 blowout reproduced in-repo, at both player counts.** Uniform won **0 of 800 games at 2P** (0 of 400 paired units) and **0 of 400 games at 4P** (0 of 100 rotation blocks). Both cells produced the typed **zero-width refusal** rather than a printed ±0.000 — which is exactly what the bracket pair exists to exercise (spec §0-R3(iv)): θ̂ = 0.000000 with zero per-unit variance is not an interval, and the honest statement is the complementary one-sided bound.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2P uniform vs retreat-averse | 400 pairs | 800 | 0.000000 | **REFUSED (zero width)** | rule-of-three 3/400 = **0.0075** | **−118.76** |
| 4P uniform vs retreat-averse | 100 blocks | 400 | 0.000000 | **REFUSED (zero width)** | rule-of-three 3/100 = **0.030** | **−75.24** |

**Comparability, stated rather than assumed.** The 4P cell matches E2's N exactly (400 games across 100 rotation blocks). The 2P cell is **400 pairs / 800 games against E2's 2000 pairs / 4000 games** — the N the spec §11 deliverable specifies, a fifth of E2's. So the 2P row reproduces the SHAPE at the specified N; it is not a same-N replication, and the honest reading of "0/800" is its rule-of-three bound (uniform's true 2P win rate against this opponent is under 0.75% with 95% confidence), not "0%". The mean VP margin is the closer comparison: **−118.76 here against E2's recorded 117.8**, on an independent master.

**Null references differ by player count and are printed per cell** — 0.5 at 2P (one X against one Y), **0.25 at 4P** (ONE X seat against three Y seats, the single-champion shape). A 4P θ̂ read against 0.5 would understate the result; the report prints the right null on the row.

**Seat-bias bookkeeping** (why the swap/rotation is mandatory, spec §4): 2P seat 0 won 400/800 — exactly half, which is what a fully seat-balanced design should produce — with **0 tie-decided games in 800**, so the +0.63pp tie-break award did not arise at all in this cell. At 4P seat 0 won 98/400 against a 100/400 uniform expectation, with 6 top-VP ties (0.0150), 2 decided for seat 0.

**End-trigger rates** are recorded per cell as a by-product (these are MIXED-policy cells — one uniform seat against retreat-averse seats — so they are NOT the uniform baseline the frozen ceiling was derived from and must not be read as such):

### Why the preserved brackets are non-comparable, and what that buys

Both cells refusing an interval is the DESIGNED outcome, not a shortfall. Uniform and retreat-averse are the two rungs that existed before 3.2, and they fail in opposite directions — uniform over-samples retreats and wanders (2,161 turns/game at 4P); retreat-aversion never volunteers one and so *reaches* the states where the retreat product materializes at six figures. They bracket the design space; they do not measure anything inside it. That is precisely why spec §0-R3(i) requires the arena's non-degenerate cell to be measured against **the rung in between** — retreat-averse vs the shipped family-weighted default, which `arena_gate` measures at θ̂ 0.4725, bootstrap [0.4200, 0.5225] — and why the guaranteed edge is manufactured from two shipped `FamilyWeighted` knob settings rather than from this pair.

## 3. L11 discharge — route labels under the shipped policy

**Ledger L11** (map §8 row 12) recorded that every organic figure of the 3.1b round was measured under uniform-random play, and required 3.2/3.3 to re-measure under their own policy. This is that re-measure.

**How it was done (implementer's call, stated):** `examples/route_cost.rs` gained a `--policy` argument selecting block A's move-selection driver, rather than a separate binary. The default is unchanged — the legacy uniform-over-`get_legal_moves` driver every committed 3.1b figure was recorded under — so an argument-free run still reproduces those curves, and it did: **225,637 decision points, 6,297 President-on-board, labels mean 3 / p99 15 / max 38, 5 allocations**, every deterministic figure identical to the 3.1b close. `--policy family-weighted` drives the same sweep through a `DecisionHandle` with `RolloutPolicy::FAMILY_WEIGHTED_DEFAULT`, the arena's raw-state driver shape.

### What this settles

**The 12.3% on-board share was a policy artifact, and it is now MEASURED rather than asserted.** The 3.1b close said so in prose ("uniform play returns the President to hand far more than competent play, so a real player pays the walk enumeration at a higher rate"). Under the shipped policy that share is **73.5% — a 6.0× move** — so a real player's search pays the route walk at six times the rate uniform play suggested. Any 3.3 cost model sized off uniform's on-board rate is sized off an artifact.

**The organic label maximum is policy-conditional, and no ceiling is known.** In-repo the observed max moves **38 → 66** (p99 15 → 21) purely from changing the driving policy on the same seeds. E1's held-out screening data recorded **205** at 4P under the same shape at n=2000/cell — larger again, because N is ~55× larger, not because the shapes disagree. All three are **maxima OBSERVED under a named harness**, never ceilings (ledger L9's permanent qualification). The practical consequence for 3.3: a widening schedule sized off "organic max 38" is sized off a uniform-policy artifact at small N, and the dense adversarial cells (458/519/612/694) remain the only figures in the same order as a real worst case.

**The cost shape is unchanged and the bound still has headroom.** Allocations per call stay at mean 5 in both arms; the dense cells are fixture-driven and measured identically under both (458 labels / 467 allocations against the frozen reference's 1,687,537 — 54.1× on latency, 3,613× on allocations). The `≤ 2·labels + 32` whole-family bound is unthreatened by the policy change.

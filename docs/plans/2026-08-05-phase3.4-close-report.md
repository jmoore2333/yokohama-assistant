## 6. What 3.4 leaves OPEN, deliberately

**(a) The eight reversal conditions of the decision itself** (ADR §8, quoted in shape, not re-argued here): evaluator v2 (the finding is JOINT with `WeightTable::V1`); **a properly tuned MCTS-v1 — the prior/proposal-quality class, which the ADR names as the mechanism most likely carrying this result**; budget regimes an order of magnitude above the 750–2,100 band; the paired d=4 transposition cell; candidate (c) with its positive control as a hard prerequisite; the unspent C5 ladder walk; a node-matched cell that matches *realized* spend rather than the cap; and player count / K. Each is a measurement, not an argument.

**(b) The world-sensitivity measurement AT THE EXPERIMENT'S OPERATING POINT — an OPEN spec obligation, and it is NOT claimed discharged.** Spec §0.4 arms §7's world-sensitivity rate as candidate (c)'s standing entry instrument. What exists is the in-repo instrument's reading **0.0857 (3 of 35 non-forced points)** in its own stated regime — 2P, `W = 3`, **64 simulations**, `M = 2`, `LeafRule::HandOff`, shipped `c`/`scale`, 80 points of which 45 forced and excluded. That regime is **not** the experiment's: the registered cells run at a 750 total-scored-successor cap with `c = 0.1`, `scale = 100`, `M = 8`, and a deeper search has more descent below the hand-off and therefore more exposure. **The figure at `mcts-v1@750` does not exist**, and taking it needs either an edit to `tests/observation_leakage.rs` or a fresh out-of-repo probe. It is carried forward as a named follow-up together with **its positive-control prerequisite** — without the control, a nonzero reading cannot be split into value channel vs tree-shape dice divergence, so the instrument's SCALE is uncalibrated and **no numeric threshold is pre-registered on it, deliberately**. The consequence is small in *this* outcome only because §0.4 arms the instrument for the ADOPTED architecture, which is S1 — whose answer is the pinned structural 0/80. It is small; it is not zero; and it is not discharged.

**(c) The unspent pre-declared masters.** `0xA3E1_2026_0804_0009` (C5, reserved for the ladder walk whose trigger did not fire) and the S3 alternates `…_000A` / `…_000B` (reserved for a null-gate failure that did not occur) are recorded as **unspent**.

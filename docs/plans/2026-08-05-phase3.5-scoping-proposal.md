## 2. Three structural facts that shape everything below

Named up front because every workstream bends around them.

**(a) `lookahead-v1` is both the retained architecture and a rung 3.5 must beat.** The production bot is S1's own successor, so the gate requires it to be measurably stronger than the thing it productionizes. Consequence: **evaluator v2 + opponent-denial/end-timing depth + tuning are load-bearing for the gate, not polish** — a bare repackaging of the shipped rung cannot pass its own predecessor with a CI above 0.5. (Also honest framing for the ADR's joint-figure rule: the champion-vs-lookahead-v1 cell compares two different evaluators through the same search shape; the claim it supports is agent-level — "the production bot beats every preserved rung" — never a search-architecture claim.)

**(b) Three of the five cells will likely sweep, and the gate says "with CIs."** The search-vs-search field discriminates (all eight of R1's cells returned real intervals — *"lookahead-v1 versus ITSELF at a different beam never sweeps"*), so the champion-vs-greedy-v1 and champion-vs-lookahead-v1 cells should produce genuine two-sided intervals. But lookahead-v1 already sweeps family-weighted 200/200 (the 3.3 promotion cell fired the degeneracy valve: an all-won refusal with rule-of-three bound 0.985), and a stronger champion will sweep all three policy rungs harder. **Resolution, per the 3.3 precedent (map §8 row 19 — declared met "on the spec's §0 executable readings," no A3 owed):** the 3.5 spec's §0 pre-commits two accepted evidence shapes per cell — (i) a bootstrap interval entirely above 0.5, or (ii) an all-won `RefusedZeroWidth` whose rule-of-three bound 1−3/N = 0.985 exceeds 0.5, honestly labelled one-sided — and the owner approves that §0 (which owner-in-the-loop makes automatic). This is an executable reading of the gate, not a restatement: the demand ("statistically superior to every rung, with the uncertainty quantified") is unchanged.

**(c) Evaluator v2 is simultaneously 3.5 scope and the ADR's FIRST reversal condition.** ADR §8 item 1: *"Re-run: C2 both arms + C3 at v2, same masters, same N."* Doing v2 without the re-run leaves the architecture decision stranded on a superseded evaluator; the kickoff says re-opening is *"at the owner's discretion."* **Recommendation: run the re-check at the v2 freeze** (§4-A5) — it is ~7 minutes of compute, it keeps the ADR's finding current, and its result is recorded as evidence, with any formal re-opening of the architecture question remaining a separate owner decision. 🔶 **OD-3.**

## 4. Workstream A — the evaluator v2 protocol 🔶 OD-1, OD-2, OD-3

§1.9's own words: *"The evaluator is a material, progressing part of this — versioned, re-proven each time."* What exists: `WeightTable::V1` — sixteen features × two vectors (early/late), interpolated on `progress_ppm` (max of four end-clocks), frozen 2026-07-27 with **zero of 32 weights moved** (no sign contradicted; magnitudes explicitly *"hand-set until 3.5"*, freeze §7(e)).

### A0 — Input zero: the regime precondition (L18), with its named blocker

Every T4 figure was measured on a **Greedy-v1** corpus (2,000 2P games, 78-turn regime, 99.95% ChurchTrackFull endings). Freeze §7(b): the shipped search rung *"will inhabit a different regime… the search rung's regime should be spot-checked, not that these signs will transfer."* So v2's first act is re-running `eval_probe` under **lookahead-v1 self-play** — and there is a **known blocker the kickoff does not mention: `ProbeContestant::parse` accepts only `greedy-v1` and the three policy rungs; it cannot run a Lookahead corpus today** (eval_probe.rs:218-225). Task A0 is therefore: extend the probe with a lookahead arm (the honest driver and `LookaheadAgent` already exist — small, real code task), then generate the v2 reference corpus. Cost note: a lookahead 2P game is ~200 ms against greedy's 22 ms, so a 2,000-game corpus is ~400 s release, or a smaller N with stated width consequences. The corpus records its end-trigger mix (the ChurchTrackFull monoculture caveat travels with any AUC read).

### A1 — The four named tensions, with proposed dispositions

All from weight-freeze §4/§7 (the canonical source — the 3.3 close report lists only three; the kickoff's four is correct):

1. **The reversed hand-split ordering** (§4). Measured AUC ordering is the exact reverse of the shipped `core > on_board > excess` at every stage — but the shipped ordering is *"a non-degeneracy condition on the one-ply argmax, not a claim about predictive power"* (the alternative was measured: 0 placements, turn-cap hit). **Disposition: the one-ply inequality is a HARD CONSTRAINT on v2 tuning; the AUC ordering is evidence about magnitudes only.** Any re-pricing must keep the four one-ply cells, the ordering pin, and the mutation-proven recovery falsifier green (freeze §4's executable guards), and re-pin row 18's tie-break non-degeneracy if ties change shape.
2. **The warehouse decay** (§3.2 row 3). Separation GREW early (0.3106) and does not decay late (0.2742) while the weight decays −10 → −4. **Disposition: re-price the decay against the A0 corpus** — flatten or re-shape as measured; sign untouched.
3. **The achievement post-claim double-count** (§7(c)). A claimed card keeps paying its capped progress while its VP already landed in `victory_points`. The docs name this a **FEATURE change** (filter cards `claimed_by` the viewer out of the sum). **Disposition: in scope for v2** — it is the one term change with a named mechanism — accepting the wider blast radius a feature change carries (drift-pin expectations, goldens, scenario re-points). 🔶 **OD-1 explicitly includes/excludes this feature change.**
4. **The early-vector mixing** (§7(d)). `EVAL TOTAL` early (0.6414) separates worse than its own best member (0.6874) — *"the early vector is mixing its terms in the wrong proportions… the obvious first target."* **Disposition: the early vector is v2's primary re-pricing target.**

### A2 — New term families: opponent-denial and end-timing depth

Grown per §1.6 (rules structure + measured self-play + scenario iteration — never human strategy priors). What exists today: the evaluator's only opponent awareness is the relative form (`viewer − opponent_extreme`); opponent modeling beyond the greedy continuation was explicitly OUT of 3.3; end-timing machinery is `progress_ppm`'s four clocks (the deck clock deliberately drops the port-empty-slot conjunct). Candidate directions, each admitted only with corpus/scenario evidence and a scenario counterfactual (§5): church/customs **majority-race margins** (relative position vs the printed 6/3 and 8/4 bonuses, and vs the 2P dummy) · **achievement-race denial** (opponent proximity to claimable achievements) · **end-trigger control** (own vs opponent's ability to accelerate/brake the binding clock; the deck clock's dropped conjunct revisited) · terminal-margin awareness near game end. The ADR §8 item 2 residual is also an evaluator fact — *"186 candidates carrying only 4 distinct one-ply values"* at Placement roots — so **value separation at Placement roots** is a v2 quality target that simultaneously sharpens any future architecture re-measurement.

### A3 — The evidence standard: how "feature interactions respected" gets teeth

The probe measures **marginal AUC per feature — it cannot answer an interaction question** (inference, but structural). The map forbids monotone-ablation dogma. Proposed evidence ladder for v2, in order: (1) A0's regime-correct marginal table (signs + magnitude shapes); (2) a **constrained fitting step** on the corpus — a held-out logistic/linear re-weighting over the (possibly extended) term set with the one-ply inequality as a hard constraint, R1-2's precedent (learned re-weighting moved held-out AUC 0.5646 → 0.6714; term redesign moved 0.008 — weights are first-order) — used as *evidence for hand-set magnitudes*, not as shipped machine weights; (3) scenario counterfactuals (§5) for every new term and every reversed preference; (4) the b-ladder screen and the §8 experiment as the final arbiter (an evaluator that AUCs better but plays worse loses). **The 3.3 one-pass rule (§2.4) was an anti-circularity device for a hand-set first cut; v2's tuning is inherently iterative. Proposed replacement discipline: iterate freely against corpus + scenarios + screening cells, but (i) the held-out/train split is fixed up front, (ii) every measured-then-set constant records its command and value before use, (iii) the FREEZE is one owner-visible event after which nothing moves, and (iv) the §8 experiment runs strictly after the freeze (L19: nothing tuned after adjudicating cells run).** 🔶 **OD-1 approves this replacement of the one-pass rule for v2.**

## 6. Workstream C — the CLI wave: productionize S1 + the CLI redesign 🔶 OD-4

**Owner-ruled 2026-08-05 (rev 1):** the live gate plays FIRST, on the CLI as it stands (§10); the CLI redesign then lands INSIDE 3.5 as **one consolidated wave** with the productionization work below — after the live-gate disposition, before acceptance play — so gate ingredient 4 is played on the surface that ships. The two halves merge deliberately: they touch the same surfaces (wizard, repl, screens, the guide pair), so one wave means one review surface, one `guide_sync` rewrite, and no screen rebuilt twice.

**The redesign half's scope source:** the table's usability findings (the exposure map routes them to *"ordinary CLI work — off-map"*, so no gate or map amendment is involved) plus the owner's UX direction, tabled as a scheduled owner touchpoint after the disposition (**OD-4 ii**). One candidate headline item is already named in-tree: the v1-beta redesign note chose line-based composed frames over a raw-mode TUI and recorded *"a future PR can layer raw-mode navigation on top without undoing any of this"* — with the tradeoffs written down (crossterm dependency, a second input path to keep honest, the ~170-test `Repl::step(&str)` line-protocol story). If raw mode enters scope, it LAYERS OVER the line protocol; the piped/tested substrate stays the source of truth.

**Owner-added scope item (2026-08-05): a record-and-replay mechanic.** Two halves, both in the wave's scope list. **(a) Recording two real players:** live mode already records a full physical game (every move + reveal as atomic units, autosaved, a replayable `GameRecord`), and sim mode records two humans at one laptop with the privacy toggle — what the wave considers on top is a streamlined RECORDING posture (minimum-ceremony entry for transcribing human play; its friction list is seeded by the live gate game itself). **(b) A replay VIEWER — genuinely new:** load any record READ-ONLY and step through it (forward/back, jump to any position, the full composed frame at that position, `log` context), where today a loaded save resumes at the end to continue playing. The viewer is the evaluation instrument the owner names — browsing human games position by position — with a natural wave-scope extension of analysis-at-position (the evaluator's explain table, or a `suggest`-shaped reading, at a stepped position; carrying the live-record honesty caveat that unrevealed cards in a live record are a reconciled fiction, so any at-position search says so). **The fence, from the owner's own settled inputs:** human-game records serve COMPARISON-ONLY evaluation — bot-vs-human agreement rates, blunder finding, scenario HARVESTING (positions the owner rules canon answers for, feeding §5's row 19+ within §1.6's "scenario iteration"), and, late and optional, the map's human-game benchmarking — **never a training or tuning input** (§1.5 "no external corpus"; §1.6 "never human strategy priors"): v2's weight evidence comes from self-play corpora and constructed scenarios only. Seat provenance already distinguishes human seats (`CONTROLLER_HUMAN`), so a corpus reader filters by origin.

**Two constraints the wave carries:** the honesty net holds through any redesign (engine as sole authority · piped-plain byte-identical + `NO_COLOR` · guide pair updated in the same commit · **no new sim-record event kinds** — the event-count key underpins bot determinism and `suggest`); and **live-mode flow FREEZES once the gate passes**, except for fixes the gate's own findings demand — rebuilding the live-tracking UX right after the table validated it would spend the validation. **egui/GUI is OUT of 3.5** (the gate's own text is "through the CLI"); it is a natural post-bar track.

**The productionization half** (gate-required, unchanged from rev 0). No inherited definition exists (*"neither document anywhere DEFINES what productionizing S1 means"* — 3.4 reader, confirmed). Proposed definition, in one sentence: **the production bot is the uncapped S1 shape at v2 weights and D-tuned knobs, playable and explainable in the CLI, with provenance that round-trips.** Concretely:

1. **The production contestant** = `lookahead-v2` (S1 shape, `WeightTable::V2`, knobs from §7's tuning — ONE named configuration string). **Uncapped: the cap is an experiment instrument, not production config** — C0 measured the cap package at θ̂ 0.230 against the shipped rung, and any future latency cap would need its own C0-style certification cell (none is anticipated: latency is a non-issue, below).
2. **CLI bot seats run contestants, not rollout policies.** Today the bot seat is typed `Option<RolloutPolicy>` and hard-constant `FAMILY_WEIGHTED_DEFAULT` — and **the gate ingredient "decision explanations for every move" is unachievable with that, by construction** (Policy rungs have no `ExplainHandle` — *"explain nothing and produce no handle at all"*). So the type migrates to `Option<Contestant>`; a `Contestant` name/knob parser lands in core (today the only parser is `examples/arena.rs`'s, unimportable — a named gap); the wizard offers the production bot as the default bot seat with the rungs available as labelled options.
3. **Determinism preserved, by the suggest pattern:** a FRESH agent per decision keyed by `(bot_seed, record().events.len())` — never a long-lived agent RNG (`LookaheadAgent` holds one; adopting it naively breaks undo idempotence, resume line-continuity, and re-pump determinism). Corollary constraint on everything in 3.5: **no new non-move event types in sim records** — the event-count key also underpins `suggest`; explanations are computed on demand, never recorded as events.
4. **The explanation surface** (the gate ingredient's CLI half): a new **`why`** command — on-demand explanation of the bot's most recent move (and `why <n>` for any logged bot move), replay-derived at the position the search valued, reusing `suggest`'s public rendering (explain table, principal continuation, runner-ups). `suggest` stays own-seat/sim-only; `why` is the bot-move surface. Per-move inline dumps are deliberately NOT the design (~8 explanations × ~45 rows per bot turn would bury the human's own move — the existing `BOT_LINE_CAP` philosophy). **Proposed executable reading of gate ingredient 3:** (a) the arena identity `x_explanations == Some(x_applied_moves)` — already gated in two release binaries — extended to the production contestant's cells; (b) every CLI bot move retrievable via `why`; (c) every human move advisable via `suggest`. The one-ply-vs-valued-state distinction (`suggest` explains the position ~8 plies down the principal continuation) is stated in the rendering, and the L5 perfect-recall disclosure travels to §9.
5. **Provenance preserve-list** (all existing behaviors, kept): knobs ride the record never a bare name (absent cap emits no token); one full-width OS-entropy bot stream seed, echoed and saved; resume round-trips through `from_provenance`; unknown controller → HUMAN with a loud warning; live saves drop bot seats loudly.
6. **Latency: no perf work.** Shipped b=4 ≈ 1.2 ms/decision; a bot turn is ~8 decisions ≈ 10 ms release — three orders below perception. Even b=16 (~3.4 ms/dec) is fine.

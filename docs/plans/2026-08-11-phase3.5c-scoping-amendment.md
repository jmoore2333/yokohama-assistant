### D2 — The strength-reality checkpoint, reshaped

**Question:** approve the checkpoint as a two-layer structure — exploratory falsification now, a small frozen set later — with the four-family first wave?

**Recommendation: YES.** The handover's §5.B as drafted was a wave costed as a checkpoint (~8 frozen families with controls, pilots, and sized N, against a repo whose entire driven-behavior capability is one 40-decision segment). The reshape:

**Layer 1 — exploratory (now, cheap, never GO evidence):** unfrozen probes and pilots; the two owner diagnostic games (**after** D4's minimal W5 lands, so `why` exists and gets used); every owner-game position **recorded** (GameRecord + `why` transcripts) so findings can be re-read at the final controller instead of decaying into anecdotes. Owner-game findings enter the frozen layer only through the canon-ruling path below, never by the checkpoint agent's own judgment.

**Layer 2 — the frozen four-family first wave** (each with a declared root **generator** — a predicate over reachable roots + master + ordinal draw rule, never a hand-picked fixture; pilots consume draws 1..n, the confirmatory read a disjoint block n+1..n+m with the predicate frozen before the draw; a second disjoint block is **escrowed at freeze time** for the post-fix re-read, so a finding-driven fix can be re-tested without circularity):

1. **Already-triggered final round** — a root inside the final round where completing the seat before the start player reaches a terminal leaf, plus a nonterminal control earlier in the round. The only family with exact action ground truth (the tree to terminal is bounded — force each alternative and play out; no continuation-policy caveat). Rules-derivable; no canon ruling needed. Engine facts: trigger round completes then one full final round (`apply_move.rs` `advance_phase`); terminality authority `types/state.rs:287-310` + the view mirror `eval::view_is_terminal` + `is_hand_off`'s `is_game_over` disjunct.
2. **End-trigger firing** — accelerate/complete when the full score and true remaining-turn count support it, paired with the behind-state control that must keep building efficiently (canon ruling 15, inverted brake). Attacks the measured debt: no deficit-keyed term exists (registry 18 pins `a−b == c−d`), and 1,984/2,000 R0-L games end on the church clock.
3. **Exclusive-race opponent awareness** — one rooted position where altering the opponent's next-turn majority/race outcome is demonstrably worthwhile, plus a no-denial negative control (canon rulings 12/13: race arithmetic and awareness, never denial-as-default). This is the family that discriminates D3's cross-handoff question — the most expensive downstream decision — and the mechanics audit flagged its live confound: the opponent's masked starting order makes the evaluator systematically under-read opponents mid-game while the terminal re-derivation is exact; fixture design must account for it.
4. **Achievement proximity** — accelerate/complete one's own leg when proximity matters, no denial claim (both seats can claim). Double duty: the pre-bar trigger test for round 3's one genuine find (`achievement_race_proximity`, +0.054 EARLY / +0.094 MID over the shipped progress-sum) and the first on-policy evidence T3's filter would ever have.

**Staged, not first-wave:** delayed country/order engine value (the evaluator's home turf, no measured debt); contested Church/Customs response (already eval-ordering-covered; upgrade only if family 3 shows a gap); the building trigger (real coverage gap — `clock_ppm`'s fourth component dates no scenario — but the rarest ecology); world-choice stability (an armed instrument with positive and mutation controls **already exists** in `observation_leakage.rs` — it needs a strategically-selected root set, not a new probe, and any quoted figure carries the uncalibrated-scale caveat).

**Instrument defaults:** a driven family's default read is **binary** — does the final controller pick from the declared top-set at the frozen root across R pre-declared (seed, world) replications with at most F declared failures (exact binomial / rule-of-three) — reserving population intervals for the (named) families where a rate over roots is genuinely the claim; where sizing is needed, the minimum meaningful edge is pre-specified and never taken from a pilot (the in-source `pairs_needed_for_mme` hazard: a 0.28pp pilot-edge error cost 20 points of power), and any variance estimate is taken conservatively as an upper confidence bound rather than a pilot point value. Reply policies where the opponent's reply is material: fixed at family freeze from a declared menu (family-weighted, greedy@final, lookahead@final, plus one adversarial policy declared as a *rule over state*, never a move list), conjunction across the set, per-policy results printed — a robustness claim, not action ground truth, and labeled so. **Multiplicity, pre-declared with the families:** per-family R and allowed-failure count; across families, one family-level failure routes to disposition-ledger + D7's branch-0 triage, two or more in the same strategic class triggers the pivot. Families are labeled CONFIRMATORY or EXISTENCE-DEMONSTRATION at freeze; existence demonstrations can support only a pivot, never a GO. **Preference-vs-rules split:** any family whose pass criterion is a preference not derivable from rules arithmetic requires an owner canon ruling before it may support a GO or pivot, batched as the scenario canon batch was; rules-derivable families (terminal scoring, majority arithmetic with the D1/D5 dummy, remaining-turn counts) proceed without ceremony and are labeled as such.

**The harness** is its own named task with the standing cadence, sited in `yokohama-core` beside `game/eval_screen.rs` (in-crate cells over the public agent surface, called from tests and round scripts, never from argv/CLI/gates), with an `examples/` runner on the `eval_probe`/`eval_screen_round` precedent; **off the fifteen-gate roster** — a graduating fixture joins `scenario_suite`'s never-delete registry, not the harness. Not in `yokohama-cli`, not in `yokohama-oracle`. What it adds over existing plumbing is small and known: a play-from-supplied-`GameState`-to-terminal driver (the `drive_greedy_2p` pattern generalized — every current whole-game driver starts at `new_game`), the fixture/generator registry, named reply policies, and rooted-run record provenance. `search_world` (arbitrary roots, all root values + principal lines), `record::replay_from` (replay-to-root), `eval_probe::snapshot_game` (the worked walk), and `observation_leakage::decide_in_world` are reused, not rebuilt.

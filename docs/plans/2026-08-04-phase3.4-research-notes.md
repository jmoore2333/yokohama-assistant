## 0. The one-paragraph summary the spec is built on

**The field problem is solved, cheaply: `lookahead-v1` versus ITSELF at a different beam never sweeps.** All eight of R1's search-vs-search cells returned real two-sided bootstrap intervals — zero refusals — and the b-ladder (b = 1/2/4/8/16) is a calibrated opponent ladder spanning ≈340 Elo, the instrument that converts any sweeping result into a budget-equivalent location instead of a rule-of-three bound. On the candidate architectures the evidence runs one way: a naive wall-matched UCT with an evaluator leaf **loses to the one-ply greedy rung** (θ̂ 0.2667 [0.1667, 0.3667]) and 0/60 to `lookahead-v1:b=4` — while actually receiving ≈1.8–2.3× the opponent's in-cell wall (critic's re-labelling; the finding strengthens) — and the mechanism is measured, not guessed: UCT pays a full candidate enumeration per scored successor where S1 amortizes one over 4.46, and ~40% of its leaf values are read MID-TURN, states evaluator v1 was never calibrated on and which S1 by construction never evaluates. **Playouts are priced out at 2P by ~500×** (two streams, two arithmetics: ~2–6 simulations per decision at any S1-comparable budget against mean root branching 27), so any 3.4 MCTS arm is specified with an evaluator leaf. **The depth decision's named reversal condition is RETIRED**: a perfect, free, sound within-turn transposition table does not buy one extra atomic ply (TT-equipped d=3 still costs 1.4–2.4× the nodes and 2.3–5.0× the successors of plain d=2, the arm that already lost at θ̂ 0.1175) — Yokohama turns are near-tree-shaped (median root p50 duplicate ratio 1.000). **treant is BUILD, not adapt** (root progressive widening provably never fires — a live, silently-failing defect — plus a structural collision with the factored action source and a non-optional LGPL-3.0-only transitive dependency). S1's per-decision cost is regime-conditional (×2.13 on scored successors between lookahead-play and greedy-play states — the L18 classic, now measured) and Placement-root-dominated (94% of nodes from 15.5% of decision points), which is what any budget cap must be designed around.

## 5. Stream R5 — the treant spike (map §8 row 4)

**Identification settled, evidenced:** `treant` = github.com/patricker/treant, crates.io v0.4.0 (2026-04-14, only version), MIT, 3,198 SLoC, 31 `unsafe` blocks, 363 all-time downloads / 2 stars, a fork of the dormant `zxqfl/mcts` (LICENSE's 2018 Jacob Jackson copyright corroborates). Critic verified the packaging markers, the sha1, and both disqualifying findings independently.

**VERDICT: BUILD, with one narrow borrow.** The grounds, ranked:

1. **D1 (decisive, a live measured defect):** the root node's `stats.visits` is never incremented (`node_path.push` happens after descent; the root is never in the backprop path), so `max_children(visits)` is frozen at `f(0)` and **progressive widening never fires at the root** — measured: 1 of 186 root children visited over 3,000 playouts, returning a confident best move which is the engine's first-emitted null placement (exactly the repo's tie-break degeneracy). It fails SILENTLY, and the library's own regression test cannot catch it (asserts `>=` where the message says "more"; instrumented: `early_visited=2, late_visited=2` at 5,100 root visits, green). Critic verified at source (`search_tree.rs:675–679` vs `:723`).
2. **D2 (structural):** `available_moves(&self) -> MoveList` is owned/materialized with no RNG handle, no budget — `DecisionHandle` cannot back it, sampled sub-selection is inexpressible, and the only expressible cap saves nothing (2.534 vs 2.521 µs — the enumeration is paid then discarded). Adopting means retiring Phase 3.1a at the search boundary. The field survey shows the collision is CATEGORY-wide (every general Rust MCTS crate models "return all legal actions"), so BUILD is recommended against the actual field, not a vacuum.
3. **D3 (owner-level flag, not adjudicated here):** a non-optional transitive `vec_storage_reuse` 0.1.0 at `LGPL-3.0-only`, statically linked into a 3-platform released binary — and it buys only Vec-recycling.
4. Second silent foot-gun: UCT adds `c·explore` to the RAW i64 mean unnormalized — at eval-scale rewards the exploration constant has literally no effect (reward scales 1,000 and 1,000,000 give identical visit distributions). `eval::score` returns thousands; the terminal ladder is 1e9. Any in-repo UCT must define its value normalization explicitly.
5. **The borrow:** `PolicyRng::select_by_key` is a keyed uniform reservoir argmax — house tie-break rule done right, unprompted; reimplement the shape (~40 LoC) with attribution. (The library's own post-search selector is `max_by_key` last-wins, so the discipline is not end-to-end there.)

Reversal conditions are ranked in the report; a lazy/budgeted `available_moves` seam (#2) is the minimum — the others without it would not move the verdict. Determinism: single-threaded seeded search is byte-identical; the parallel path is NOT reproducible at a fixed seed (measured) — the crate's headline feature is the part this repo cannot use.

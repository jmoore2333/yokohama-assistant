# Phase 3.3 research notes — corpus feature separation, the greedy floor, depth semantics under K worlds

**Date:** 2026-07-26 · **Base:** master `0c624b8` (`SEMANTICS_REV` 5, Phase 3.2 merged) · **Status:** research input to the 3.3 spec — measurements, not commitments. **Method:** the 3.1/3.2 pattern — three independent research streams (Opus agents), each with an out-of-repo scratch crate path-depending on `yokohama-core` (repo verified untouched by each stream AND independently by the critic: `git status --porcelain` empty at `0c624b8`), followed by an independent critic instructed to re-run headline figures and attack methodology before any of this reaches a spec. All measured figures are release-profile on an Apple M5 10-core; wall figures are load-sensitive up to ~1.7×, deterministic counts are preferred and labelled. **Every maximum below is an observation under its named harness, never a ceiling.**

**Scratch-crate durability caveat:** the three research crates and the critic's patched clone live in session-local scratch space and do not survive the session. Their figures were independently reproduced by the critic before adoption (bit-exact where stated below, including R1's 193 MB corpus byte-identically and R2's every printed counter). Durable re-measurement instruments land in-repo as 3.3 deliverables (the spec's corpus-AUC probe and the arena contestant grammar), which is the same disposition 3.2 used for its research harnesses.

## 0. The one-paragraph summary the spec is built on

A greedy one-ply bot over even a crude hand-set evaluator **beats the shipped family-weighted rung decisively** — θ̂ 0.9292, bootstrap95 [0.9150, 0.9425] at N=600 paired seat-swapped 2P units — **but only under one specific, previously-undocumented design decision: the argmax tie-break must be a uniform draw over the tied set from the agent's own RNG stream.** The identical evaluator with a first-listed-wins tie-break *loses* (θ̂ 0.2633), because the engine's Placement family enumerates the null placement first and the evaluator ties on ~30% of decisions. This single choice silently produced a false headline in one research stream (R3's "greedy loses to random") that the critic isolated by patching only the tie-break in R3's own crate (0.3000 → 0.8900 on the same master). Everything else stacks on top: turn-boundary look-ahead is the leading depth-semantics candidate (it beats atomic-with-quiescence at what corrects to roughly equal cost, and it beats greedy head-to-head at 0.86 on completed games), K>1 belief determinization buys exactly nothing while the evaluator reads only public-plus-own-hand terms (0 divergences in 13,220 decisions; bit-identical games in two independent harnesses), the evaluator's **weights and signs are first-order while the term list is second-order** (re-weighting eval-v0's own 16 terms lifts early held-out AUC 0.5646 → 0.6714; two differently-designed term sets differ by 0.008), and the corpus's best early-game separator is the game's own end-scoring arithmetic re-derived from the view (`country_bonus_now`, AUC 0.6212 early), not victory points (0.5625 early — 45% of eventual winners are BEHIND on VP in the first third).

## 1. Stream R1 — which view-measurable features separate winners from losers

**Harness:** raw-state driver (legitimate: the bot is not the subject), `RolloutPolicy::FAMILY_WEIGHTED_DEFAULT` at every seat; features computed ONLY from `observe(state, seat)` `PlayerView`s — the feature module has no `GameState` import, so view-measurability is compiler-enforced. 2P primary corpus: **2,000 games / 396,548 view snapshots** (99.1 turns, 666.2 moves per game; 0 cap hits; master `0x52713F0C9A441E63`, SplitMix64 per-game world seeds; regenerates byte-identically — critic `cmp` clean on 193,515,424 bytes, 2.51 s wall). 4P structure-check corpus: 500 games / 526,576 snapshots. Analysis on game-aggregated cells (game × seat × stage tercile by post-hoc turn fraction) with game-clustered bootstrap (B=2,000) — per-snapshot AUCs are attenuated 0.03–0.06 by autocorrelation and are not headline figures. The critic reproduced every headline AUC to 4 decimals with an independent Mann-Whitney implementation.

**Critic corrections adopted for R1:**
- **The three-stage hard switch is NOT validated.** R1's flagship "three weight vectors, stage-selected from an observable proxy" ran ahead of its evidence: no proxy was validated. The critic measured the best available observables against post-hoc turn-fraction terciles on R1's own corpus: `g_church_free`/`g_church_trigger_gap` r = −0.7758 (3-class accuracy 0.6419 with in-sample-fitted thresholds), `g_order_deck_len` −0.7550 (0.6466), `abs_victory_points` +0.7206 (0.6667) — against a 0.3400 base rate. **A usable continuous proxy exists, but a hard 3-way switch mis-assigns ~35% of positions even with optimistically-fitted thresholds.** Consequence for the spec: stage dependence via SMOOTH INTERPOLATION over a continuous observable progress proxy, not a switch.
- **The corpus-regime precondition (promoted from follow-up to precondition).** The entire corpus is family-weighted self-play at 99.1 turns/game; the 3.3 greedy rung plays materially differently (78.9–80.8 turns, ~86% ChurchTrackFull endings). Every stage-dependent SIGN — most sharply R1-5's early-church-negative, whose mechanism is the 2P ChurchTrackFull clock — was measured in a regime the shipped bot will not inhabit. **Before evaluator v1's weights are frozen, regenerate the corpus under the 3.3 greedy rung and re-measure: early VP AUC, the church sign, and the assist_warehouse magnitude** (the harness re-runs 2,000 games in ~2.5 s, so this costs nothing). This is a spec-level precondition, not a nice-to-have.

## 3. Stream R3 — depth semantics and the K-world cost model

**Harness:** scheme ladder over the shared eval-v0 — S0 greedy-atomic; S1 turn-boundary depth-1 (complete own turn greedily/beam after each candidate first-move, evaluate at hand-off); S2 atomic depth-d with quiescence (forced chains extend free; evaluate at quiet points); S3 opponent hand-off (opponent completes its turn greedily inside MY sampled world — my model of them, honest by construction). Screening cells at N=30–50 units. Critic verdict: **REVISE** — measurement hygiene excellent (every re-run row reproduced exactly), but the ladder inherited an undocumented first-wins tie-break (`agent.rs` root argmax, `greedy_step`, and the beam's stable-sort truncation), which contaminated the headline conclusions. The corrected picture below folds in the critic's single-variable isolation runs (patched clone, control arm reproduces R3 verbatim; treatment changes ONLY the tie-break).

**What stands as published (tie-break-independent):**
- **K=1.** Aggregate-vs-world-0 divergence **0/13,220** K>1 decisions (S0 and S1 at K∈{3,5}); K=3 vs K=1 played identical games in all 100 head-to-head (zero-width interval, correctly REFUSED, which is the divergence result restated). Structural reason shared with R2: no eval-v0 term reads a field the sampler determinizes. **Revisit trigger, recorded:** K>1 becomes live the moment an evaluator term reads the order deck, tech deck, boxed pile, or an opponent's hidden starting order — and the acceptance test for any K knob is DIVERGENCE RATE (cheap, deterministic), never a win-rate cell, which is unmeasurable by construction at zero divergence.
- **Hand-off length:** a 2P player-turn is 6.1–7.9 moves mean, p99 ≤ 12, max observed 14 — the tight quantity that sizes turn-boundary cost (vs atomic's C^d in the Placement branching factor, where S2(d=3) reached 140,226 nodes on one decision and 26,225 nodes/decision mean at ~31 ms/decision).
- **L10 arithmetic:** handles/decision = K × per-world handles, verified exactly (S0 1.00/3.00/5.00 and S1(B=1) 56.53/169.62/282.74 at K=1/3/5).
- **Cache shapes** (adopted for the spec, constants to be re-measured): `DecisionHandle` borrows `&GameState`, so a search node cannot own one — a node owns `(GameState, Vec<Move> candidates, i64 eval)` with the handle as a scoped temporary; the bounded candidate Vec is small (mean ~14, p99 186, max observed 396) while the raw retreat family is 59,058 at 2P and must never be materialized. Cache the FORCED answer (≈60% of decision points offer ≤1 candidate, and discovering that costs a full enumeration — S2's largest line item, partly inflated by a duplicate-handle defect the critic located in R3's own S2 at pending-choice depth-0 states). Never build K per-world trees (0/13,220). Budget node memory from touched states per decision (265–1,591 for the shallow schemes), not depth.
- **Divergence is not value:** B=4 changes the move vs B=1 7.25% of the time and wins the head-to-head 0.75 — a small divergence rate can carry a large strength edge (contrast K: 0.00% divergence, zero edge, by construction).

**Open decision #2 status: turn-boundary is the LEADING CANDIDATE, not yet resolved.** The direction survives and strengthens under correction (S2 loses 0.1667 on completed games at ~equal measured cost), across two harnesses — but the decisive rows are 30-unit screening cells, the critic's beam patch did not randomize the beam's internal stable-sort truncation, and ledger row 16 requires sizing from a pre-specified MME. **The spec pre-registers the confirming experiment** (cost-matched S2(d=2) vs S1(B=4) rematch, fully-randomized tie-breaks, completed games, MME-sized) and 3.3 execution runs it before the search rung is frozen — which is exactly the map's "settled here empirically".

## 4. Cross-stream facts and open questions

1. **The headline contradiction and its resolution** (the package's most important finding): R2 and R3 measured the same configuration at 0.93 and 0.30 because R3's argmax tie-break was first-wins and undocumented. Under matched tie-breaks the two independently-written harnesses agree in BOTH arms (first-wins 0.2633 vs 0.3000; uniform 0.9292 vs 0.8900). **Consequence: the tie-break is a spec-level requirement with its own pin** (a constant-valued evaluator must not produce 100%-empty-placement games), not an implementation detail.
2. **Cost model is coherent:** R2's isolates compose to 21.3 µs/decision for S0; R3 measures 21.4 independently (6% agreement). Search that re-observes per node is observe-bound (observe = 45% of candidate cost) — the strongest argument shaping the cache design.
3. **Convergent eval-v0 defect, two independent instruments:** R2 (behavior): nonzero-recovery retreats can never be argmax under on_board(18)/in_hand(8) — 0/48,458, reproducing the RetreatAverse pathology `policy_diversity`'s gate exists to detect, from the evaluator side. R1 (outcomes): `assist_warehouse` is the largest-magnitude NEGATIVE term and `assist_in_hand` strongly positive. Evaluator v1 needs explicit warehouse/recovery pricing, and **the M-is-a-no-op finding is eval-v0-conditional and must not harden into the design** — under v1 weights recovery becomes attractive and retreat candidates matter again; the falsifier is a re-measured nonzero-recovery pick share under v1 (must NOT be 0.0000).
4. **The 4P placement cap is KNOWN to bind:** the repo's own committed curve (`cargo run --release -p yokohama-core --example action_cost`) realizes **3,231 placement combos on the shipped 4P+Newspaper fixture — 6.3× the 512 cap** — so the sampling fallback arm is the arm that fires at 4P and it has never executed in any measurement either stream made. 2P (the gate count) is safe (max realized 396); the fallback needs dedicated coverage before the scheme is called settled.
5. **Relative vs absolute:** R1 (separation) says relative wins ~1.5×; R2 (play) says indistinguishable at 2P — not a conflict (at 2P a relative shift is near-constant per decision and constants cannot move an argmax). Ship relative; the equivalence is 2P-only (at 4P mean-of-three-opponents washes out the leader).
6. **The end-timing confound, one open question rather than two disclaimers:** greedy's wins come with games ending ~12 turns earlier, ~86% via ChurchTrackFull; R1's early-church-negative was measured under a policy that never times the track deliberately. The same mechanism read from both sides, unresolved by design here: the §1 corpus-regime precondition re-measure (under greedy play) is the first instrument that can separate "faster trigger-racing" from "better play"; the scenario suite should carry an end-timing scenario either way.
7. **No stream violated the honesty boundary** (critic-verified by code read): all evaluators/features typed over `&PlayerView`; sampler refusal is a hard error with no fallback everywhere; R1's raw-state corpus driver is legitimate (the bot is not its subject) with compiler-enforced view-measurability.

## 5. What the spec may build on / must not build on (critic's closing, adjudicated and adopted)

**Build on:** the tie-break rule (spec-level, pinned); the greedy rung's strength (two independent harnesses); K=1 + the revisit trigger + divergence-rate-as-acceptance-test; the cost model (observe-bound search; 21 µs greedy decisions; corrected ~1.0–1.1 ms/decision for B=4 turn-boundary); R1's feature analysis (weights/signs first-order; country_bonus_now; warehouse-negative; smooth stage interpolation over a validated-in-passing continuous proxy); the convergent recovery-pricing defect; the cache shapes.

**Must not build on:** R3's published "greedy loses to random" and the tempo-term-or-scored-CapReached forced choice (both tie-break artifacts, withdrawn); the published 2:1 S1:S2 cost margin (corrected ~1.08:1 — a spec budgeting turn-boundary at ~700 µs/decision would be ~50% under; budget ~1.0–1.1 ms at B=4); R3's structural constants as sizing inputs (re-measure under the shipped tie-break); R2's withdrawn "4 of 108,000" sentence; any 4P claim about the 512 placement cap; a hard three-stage weight switch (~35% mis-assignment at the measured proxy accuracy); reading the greedy floor θ̂ 0.9292 as a statement about tuned strength (it is a floor for THIS placeholder weight vector under THIS opponent).

**Sizing note:** every strength cell here is a research probe at budget-chosen N (600/200/50/30), none MME-sized; the only sizing precedent the spec inherits is the 3.2 spec §4 promotion-cell doctrine (MME 0.60 → 200 pairs / 400 games from measured Var_pair), reiterated by the owner's 3.3 kickoff.

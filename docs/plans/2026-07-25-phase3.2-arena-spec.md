## §0 Gate restatements (the load-bearing ruling — owner-visible, map §11 amendment A2)

The map §5-3.2 gate reads: *"arena reproducibly ranks two different seat policies with honest intervals; the fuzzer runs the weighted policy and 4P trigger diversity measurably recovers (ChurchTrackFull > 0%, option-count tails bounded); emitted records replay strict-clean."* Three clauses cannot serve as acceptance criteria as written — each shown by measurement, each independently by two harnesses where noted. Per the critic's routing this is an **owner-visible disposition**, adopted under the 3.0–3.4 autonomy envelope with both clean-eyes lenses instructed to attack it FIRST (the A1 precedent, 3.1b §0); it lands at three sites in this PR (this section, map §11 as amendment A2, a guidingpath decision-log row). The v1 text of R1 and R2 carried defects both lenses caught (a false conjunct, an unproven impossibility, an unsized flaky gate, a bound-for-observation swap with no replacement pin); this v2 text is the amendment text.

**R1 — "ChurchTrackFull > 0%" does not discriminate.** Uniform itself satisfies it: 1/500 = 0.20% (95% Wilson [0.04%, 1.12%]) at M1's harness; 7/2000 = 0.35% [0.17%, 0.72%] on E1's held-out master-B set — two independent harnesses. (The recorded "0.0% at 200 games" was an N artifact — reproduced: 0/200 at M1's probe.) **Restated:** at 4P, the shipped family-weighted policy's ChurchTrackFull **95% Wilson LOWER bound exceeds a FROZEN uniform ceiling of 0.72%** — the held-out uniform upper bound (7/2000, E1 master B `16045690984833335230`, N=2000, release, raw-state screening driver), cited as a constant with full provenance printed on the gate's report row. The gate cell runs the weighted policy fresh at **N=500** (computed failure probability ≈0.2% at the held-out true rates; expected margin: lower bound ≈2.4% vs 0.72%). A two-sided disjoint-intervals design with a fresh uniform comparator was REJECTED by simulation: it flakes 9.1–9.3% of runs at N=500 and needs N≥750–1000 per cell to be non-flaky, whose comparator alone costs 28–56s (both lenses, independent computations agreeing). A fresh uniform 4P trigger rate is still RECORDED at every close by the §11 A/B cell — a drift watch on the frozen constant, never the assert's comparator.

**R2 — "option-count tails bounded" mis-aims the criterion, and the honest replacement is a recorded observation PLUS a hard cost bound.** No shape in E1's 28-shape screen (masters A+B, 2/3/4P, N=1000+2000/cell, release, raw-state driver) that measurably fixed the 4P collapse kept tails below six figures, and the mechanism explains why: on-board assistant density is simultaneously what makes play score-productive and what inflates the retreat product ∏(count+1) — one variable, two symptoms. Even flat p=0.5, which barely moves the trigger, reached 165,903 options; the recommended shape observed 186,634 (held-out); the sweep's largest cell was 331,828 = 75% of the known reachable 442,384 (shape `low3_p=0.3333` — outside the shipped box — 4P, n=1000, screening master `6435445196422466881`). **This is a screened observation with a mechanism, not an impossibility theorem — no bound is claimed and none is known** (the map §8 row 9 register). Under the factored surface the tail is a counting observation, not a cost: the recommended shape costs 2.48 µs/decision, 3.05× cheaper than uniform's 7.58 µs (held-out N=2000/cell, raw-state fuzzer-shape driver — no validation, no invariants; the arena's own per-decision cost is measured in-plan and is NOT this figure). **Restated:** option-count tails are **RECORDED** (median/p90/p99/max, per player count × phase × policy, each with policy/seeds/N/profile stated) and the **per-decision cost is bounded, executably**: (a) the policy **never materializes the exhaustive union at a Movement decision point** — the exploding retreat and routes families are never served through `get_legal_moves` (the delegated single-family phases DO call it underneath, by the module's documented design; no exploding family lives at those phases) — pinned by the §8 L10 counter at Movement points **with the President on board** (a stray `get_legal_moves` there runs a second route DFS, count 2), and at **in-hand** Movement points — where the routes family emits `PlacePresident` without walking, so the counter is blind, yet the same exploding retreat family is dispatched (T2 review: 88.6% of organic Movement points are in-hand under uniform play) — by the (b) pick bound, whose fixture set includes an in-hand cell for exactly this reason; and (b) a **per-edit, never-`#[ignore]`d hard allocation-COUNT bound on ONE `RolloutPolicy` pick** at the 4P OnBoard 442,368-move fixture the equivalence gate already owns — anti-vacuity first (pin the realized `retreat_len` = 442,368 and the realized route-label count against the frozen reference on the SAME fixture), then meter; bytes/wall recorded, never asserted; mutation-proven by routing the pick through `get_legal_moves` (3.1a/3.1b's meter precedent, same binary so the counting allocator is not duplicated — §8).

**R3 — "reproducibly ranks two different seat policies with honest intervals" is satisfiable while exercising no interval machinery.** The two policies that exist are a blowout: retreat-averse beat uniform **4000/4000 games** at 2P (2000 paired+swapped pairs) and **400/400 games across 100 rotation blocks** at 4P — θ̂=1.000 with zero per-pair variance, so t/bootstrap intervals silently collapse to ±0.000 (E2, verified at source output). **Restated — the arena gate requires, together:** (i) at least one **non-degenerate** cell with a measured edge reporting a real interval — first candidate retreat-averse vs family-weighted; E1's paired-VP data suggests that pair may measure near-null at 2P (dVP −0.50 ± 1.73), which the gate ANTICIPATES as a valid outcome — the guaranteed edge cell is **two shipped `FamilyWeighted` knob settings** (p=0.05 vs p=1/6 at k=5 — E1 measured them apart), manufactured from shipped machinery and labelled as a diagnostic cell; (ii) at least one **same-policy null cell** with θ̂ inside its interval around 0.5 and a nonzero SE; (iii) **validation of the interval machinery** in two honest parts: the estimator arithmetic (Wilson/t/bootstrap/refusal) validated on **synthetic unit outcomes with known truth** at R ≥ 2000 (no games played — free and genuinely non-vacuous), plus ONE small real-game replicate block checking empirical sd(θ̂) against the reported SE within a band derived from that block's R (binomial CI stated) — the clustering check that only real games buy; (v) **reproducibly** = re-running the arena with the same recorded master and spec reproduces the tabulation byte-identically — gate cells run **single-threaded** on a **committed diagnostic master** (§3's exemption), so this pin holds by construction; parallelism is an evaluation-run option, not a gate-cell one.

**What is NOT restated:** "the fuzzer runs the weighted policy" (it does, at 2/3/4P — §7); "emitted records replay strict-clean" (kept verbatim, made executable — §5); the gate's purpose (strength claims possible; the fuzzer promise paid). ## §2 D1 — the policy module (`game/policy.rs`)

**`RolloutPolicy`** (name final at plan time; one enum, `Copy`, `Eq`, serde):

- `Uniform` — exact uniform over the legal union, served families-first: at the one multi-family decision point (Movement) the two family sizes are EXACT (`retreat_len` closed form; the collected routes `Vec` length), so a two-stage draw weighted by exact sizes is exactly uniform over the union; at every single-family point, uniform-within-family (placement via `placement_sample`'s reservoir = exact uniform over realized moves; Placement's `UpperBound` never enters a weight because Placement is single-family). The chain was source-verified by both lenses; **the new composition gets its own executable pin** (the existing sampler-uniformity pins cover only the within-family samplers): a per-edit chi-square/total-variation test at a constructed Movement decision point and a fee-gated Placement point, comparing the families-first Uniform's empirical distribution against uniform-over-`get_legal_moves`.
- `RetreatAverse` — difftest semantics exactly: never volunteer a retreat. At a Movement point the pool is routes ∪ {**the single all-zero** `ReturnPresidentToHand`} — **when routes are structurally empty the ALL-ZERO recovery vector is forced, never a nonzero-recovery vector** (the pool is never empty, so difftest's full-list fallback is unreachable at Movement); uniform elsewhere. Two pins: **zero nonzero-recovery retreats applied** across N driven games (the property M1 measured as the rung's identity — 0 of 345,800 applied 4P moves at its 200-game cell; 0 of 870,350 at 500), and a **same-state agreement pin against difftest's `pick_move`** (given the same state and legal list, the two implementations select the same move from the same RNG stream — E2's whole-game fidelity pin is the working model).
- `FamilyWeighted { retreat_ppm: u32, recovery_min_of: u8 }` — E1's two-knob shape. Knob 1: P(retreat family | Movement decision point) = `retreat_ppm`/10⁶, default **166_667** (p = 1/6). Knob 2: each area's recovery count = **min of k iid U{0..=count} draws**, default k = **5** — full support (every recovery vector including the maximal one keeps positive probability; retreat-averse's zero interior coverage is the disqualifying defect it fixes), constructed via `retreat_move`, O(#areas). Routes structurally empty → the weighted retreat draw proceeds; elsewhere uniform-within-family. The defaults are **the best-measured cell** (`low5_0.1667`: held-out 4P ChurchTrackFull 3.7% [2.96, 4.62]) inside E1's validated box p ∈ [0.05, 0.20] × k ∈ [3, 5]; the acceptance criteria (§0-R1, §7, §12) gate the shape, not the constants.
- Structural honesty note carried in the module doc: `families()` returns >1 tag at exactly ONE decision-point shape (Movement: `[MovementRoutes, MovementRetreat]`) — verified by code read and E1's 133,800-game executable guard — so "family weighting" is precisely a routes-vs-retreat scalar plus within-family weighting, and a per-family weight TABLE is deliberately not built (E1 F1). A debug assertion pins the two-tag shape so a future third tag fails loudly instead of silently skewing the draw.
- The pick function consumes ONLY the factored surface via the `DecisionHandle` (§8), holding L10 (one routes enumeration per decision point) and never materializing the exploding families; the delegated single-family phases call the frozen generator underneath by design (§0-R2's honest statement). Seat-blind; `&mut impl Rng` in; pure over (handle, rng).
- **Sampler-side only:** the engine's legal list is never weighted (map §5-3.2 item 3, settled).
- **difftest:** its local `Policy` enum is deleted and re-exported from core with **string names preserved** (`"uniform"`/`"retreat-averse"` — three checked-in artifacts carry them in filename and payload). Its list-based `pick_move` **must change** (a third enum variant makes the match non-exhaustive — v1's "body UNCHANGED" was wrong, lens-caught): the `FamilyWeighted` arm returns a typed error ("not supported by the list-based difftest driver — use the core arena"), and difftest's CLI parser keeps accepting exactly its two names, so the arm is unreachable from its subcommands and its streams/sweeps/recorded figures stay exactly reproducible. The oracle-independence rule does not bite (it binds the oracle crate; policies are driver-side).

**Honesty thread (map §3):** the leakage suite runs against the new policy — the canary-armed decision-invariance harness gains the family-weighted agent, plus two non-vacuous additions (M3): a **seed-provenance canary** (prove the harness catches a policy seeded from a hidden-state fingerprint) and **weight-vector invariance** under hidden variation (strictly stronger than pick invariance; the exposed weights are the two knobs plus the two exact family sizes). These two, plus §10.3's T4 factored-surface extension, are the executable evidence for §4's per-family invariance requirement — all three are per-edit tier, load-bearing, listed in §12.

## §3 D2 — seed hygiene (`game/seeds.rs`)

The operational closure of the seed-oracle residual (map §8 row 8), enforced at the type level:

- `WorldSeed(u64)` newtype; **the arena's public surface takes `WorldSeed`, never a bare `u64`.**
- `SeedSchedule` — SplitMix64 stream over a master; per-game seeds are full-width derivations, recorded per game (in `GameRecord.seed`, as today) and per run (master in provenance + report). Paired designs derive ONE `WorldSeed` per pair/block.
- **The master comes from OS entropy per bot-facing EVALUATION run** and is recorded — a compile-time master would collapse the seed-oracle search space to the index range (critic #4; E1/E2 practiced full-width-from-fixed-literal, which is reproducibility, not entropy — stated in their figures). `SeedSchedule::diagnostic(master)` exists for reproduction and fixtures, and its name is the waiver (M2's naming rule); re-running a recorded evaluation via its recorded master is the intended reproduction path.
- **Gate-cell exemption (lens-caught collision):** every asserting cell in `arena_gate`/`policy_diversity` runs on `SeedSchedule::diagnostic(<committed master>)` — a regression test wants reproducibility, which is exactly what the constructor's name declares; an OS-entropy gate would flake at ≥α by construction (the null cell fails at nominal rate on fresh entropy) and would break R3(v)'s determinism pin. The OS-entropy rule binds published/bot-facing evaluation runs — the ones whose figures get quoted.
- **Corpus regeneration is distributional by design** under a fresh master; bit-identical regeneration = a diagnostic re-run against the recorded master. Stated in the emitter's doc so "regenerates by script" is not misread as byte-identity.
- **No value-based seed checks anywhere** (spec-level prohibition, M2 #7): any u64 is a legitimate draw; the enforced property is the DERIVATION, not the value. A reviewer adding `assert!(seed > 2^32)` is adding false comfort — this sentence exists to be cited against that.
- The four existing `base_seed + i` sites (selfplay, three difftest sweeps) are QUARANTINED, not rerouted: `wrapping_add` + a comment declaring them diagnostic sweeps, never bot-facing evaluation (rerouting would silently change the seed sets behind published gate figures). Unit fixtures and existing gates keep small literal seeds — the rule binds bot-facing evaluation, exactly as `agent.rs`'s SEED HYGIENE prose already says; this lands it in types.
- `t8f_red_team_the_public_board_still_recovers_a_small_setup_seed` stays green and untouched — the executable reminder the kickoff names.

## §4 D3 — the arena (`game/arena.rs` + `examples/arena.rs`)

**Home: core.** Grounds (M1, critic-confirmed): the weighted policy must be in core for fuzzer reach (settled); the fuzz gate and `selfplay` see only core's public API (the `#[path]` include mechanism); the CLI deps on core alone so `play vs bot` costs zero new dependencies; difftest's separate-crate precedent exists to quarantine the oracle dep, which the arena lacks.

**The agent seam (lens-caught; named honestly):** the arena does NOT inherit `HonestAgent` — that trait's `choose_move` takes a materialized `&[Move]`, which is exactly the ceiling this driver exists to avoid, and `run_honest_game`'s per-seat routing lives inside that function. The arena defines its own interface, **`ArenaAgent`**: `choose_starting_order(&mut self, seat, deal: &[OrderCardId; 2]) -> u8` (the same shape as `HonestAgent`'s method, so 3.0's structural coverage of the setup decision carries over as a DESIGN, re-implemented here) and `choose_move(&mut self, view: &PlayerView, handle: &DecisionHandle, rules: &Rules) -> Move` — families-first, K>1-ready (an implementation may sample several worlds internally; 3.3 inherits the cheap surface). `HonestAgent`/`run_honest_game` stay untouched (3.0 surface with its own tests). The arena **re-implements the factory-inside-the-driver discipline** (`make_agent(seat, players)` invoked by the driver after `new_game`, so no caller can construct an agent from privileged setup data). **Executable pin for the kickoff obligation:** two seats with distinguishable setup-choice agents produce the expected distinguishable `GameRecord.choices`, and the emitted record replays strict-clean (per-edit).

**Driver — families-first, honest, non-materializing (critic #10 settled here):**
- Per decision: `observe(true_state, seat)` → the seat's agent determinizes ONE world (`BeliefSampler::from_view`, seed drawn from the agent's OWN rng stream — never a state-flavoured value) → `DecisionHandle` over the world → `RolloutPolicy` pick → **`move_legality::is_move_legal(true_state, &mv, rules, PathMode::Canonical)`** — design A doing the job open decision #3 settled it for — → `apply_move_unchecked` → record the event. An `is_move_legal` false is a HARD error (arena bug), mirroring `run_honest_game`'s illegal-move discipline.
- **Sampler failure is a typed HARD error — no fallback** (lens-caught trap: the in-tree `TrivialHonestAgent` precedent silently falls back to the TRUE legal list on `from_view` error, i.e. privileged play recorded only in a tally; an arena inheriting that would emit privileged-play games into the corpus 3.3 trains on, invisibly to the leakage suite). The honest-path counters become **gate assertions**: over `arena_gate`'s games, sampled-path count == decision count and fallback count == 0.
- **Membership cross-check (debug tier):** `PathMode::Canonical` is deliberately wider than list membership (it does not verify lex-least witness selection), so the arena driver additionally asserts `chosen ∈ get_legal_moves(true_state)` under `debug_assertions`, scoped to the arena driver only — the per-edit run catches a non-canonical-witness regression cheaply; the RELEASE-side closing authority is the corpus strict-replay check (§5), which pushes every emitted move through the checked `apply_move` boundary.
- **Leak-freedom, stated precisely (lens-tightened):** the property the weighted draw needs is **per-family invariance** — identical family tags AND identical exact sizes across every world consistent with the seat's view — which is stronger than union-set equality. Evidence: the T4 view-measurability result + the union+order equivalence, made executable for the factored surface by §10.3's T4 extension (families/iter/len invariance across hidden variants and sampled worlds) and §2's weight-vector-invariance leakage pin. Both are load-bearing per-edit gates (§12), not optional ride-alongs.
- `sample_family` → `None` (structurally empty family): remove that family from the candidate set and re-draw among the rest — never retry (a retry is a second enumeration); at a single-family point, `None` = dead end = typed hard error (M3 #8).
- Per-move `check_invariants` stays ON (every existing driver's contract; quiet-load measured cost is built into the §12 cells — uniform 4P 500-game cell 27.9s WITH per-move invariants) with a documented off-switch for bulk corpus generation, whose records are verified by strict replay instead.
- v1 is **K=1 determinization** (one world per decision). K>1 aggregation is 3.3's business; `ArenaAgent`'s shape admits it.
- A **raw-state mode** (policy over the true state, no view/sampler) exists for the fuzzer and labelled diagnostics only — and the label rides the ARTIFACT, not prose (§5's `observation` field; map §1.1's "explicitly labeled diagnostic" is owner-settled). A raw-state run either refuses the corpus dir or stamps every record `full-state-diagnostic`; pinned executably.

**Design (pairing, rotation, keying — E2 throughout):**
- 2P: the pair is the unit — one `WorldSeed`, both seatings. 3–4P: the rotation block is the unit (4 games at 4P). Seat swap is **bias removal** (unswapped arrangement gap measured up to 3.6pp) and is mandatory; seed pairing is kept as bookkeeping and bit-identical-start reproducibility but **never claimed as variance reduction** (measured: 0–2.5% at 2P, −13% at 4P). 3P seat symmetry is UNTESTED (E2 caveat) — 3P cells are supported but carry that caveat until measured.
- **Keying rule (the CRN trap):** setup streams keyed by (world_seed, seat) — paired arrangements share a bit-identical post-setup state, pinned; move/agent streams keyed by (world_seed, seat, **contestant-slot**) — keying by seat alone made a null cell exactly degenerate (ρ=−1.0000, SE 0.000: an instrument that reads "perfectly calibrated" while measuring nothing). The null-cell self-test catches regressions of this rule by construction.
- Start-player: `GameConfig::for_player_count` pins seat 0; the tie-break's seat-0 AWARD is +0.63pp under the brackets — derived as half the 1.26% tie rate (pooled 20,000 seat-balanced 2P games: seat-0 wins 0.5070, non-tied 0.5007; the derivation stated so it is not mistaken for E2's +0.70pp total-advantage headline). Seat rotation of contestants across the pair/block neutralizes it at the tabulation level; the per-rung tie rate is recorded (it scales the bias one-for-one and will move under stronger bots).

**Tabulation and intervals (E2's discipline, adopted whole):**
- Outcome: wins via `final_result` (the official winner API); seat-balanced VP margin recorded alongside as the screening/triage statistic (1.4–2.1× fewer pairs for equal power) — the **win rate is the gate statistic** ("plays to win").
- Primary interval: **percentile bootstrap over the design unit** (pair/block), B=10,000, resampling seed recorded; unit-level t as the cheap default; Wilson-on-pooled-games printed as a cross-check and NEVER primary at 3–4P (clustering: 4P ρ=+0.051 → ~93.2% true coverage).
- **Zero-width refusal:** sd(unit outcomes) == 0 → typed refusal + one-sided Clopper-Pearson/rule-of-three bound, never ±0.000 (the bracket pair is the standing test vector).
- **Fixed N, sized from a pre-specified minimum meaningful edge** — never from a pilot point estimate (measured hazard: 20 points of power lost to a 0.28pp pilot error). Defaults from measured variance: promotion cells MME 0.60 → 200 pairs / 400 games; milestone-precision cells h=0.02 → ~1,189 pairs (3.5's business, documented now; lens re-derivation from printed Var_pair: 1,182–1,207). No peeking (8 naive looks measured 19.0% type-I); if early stopping is ever wanted it spends alpha explicitly — recorded as a rule.
- **Standing self-tests:** the null cell (θ̂ within interval of 0.5, SE nonzero) and the determinism pin (single-threaded gate cells on a committed diagnostic master → byte-identical report).
- **Run-failure taxonomy (lens-tightened):** a driver error is a BUG (fails the run loudly, emits nothing); a MAX_TURNS cap hit is a DISTINGUISHED outcome — it also fails the asserting cell and emits nothing, but the report prints the seed and names it "cap reached" so it is reproducible (uniform tails run to 5,364 of the 10,000 cap in E1's held-out cells — nonzero risk on uniform baseline/bracket cells only).

## §5 D4 — records, provenance, corpus (`GameRecord.run` + emission)

- **One provenance field** serving arena AND CLI (M4's unification): `#[serde(default, skip_serializing_if = "Option::is_none")] pub run: Option<RunProvenance>` on `GameRecord`, modelled on `lockdown` (the in-tree replay-inert precedent). `RunProvenance { schema: u32, master_seed: Option<u64>, run_id: Option<String>, seats: Vec<SeatProvenance> }`; `SeatProvenance { controller: String, version: u32, params: BTreeMap<String, i64>, agent_seed: Option<u64>, observation: String }` — `controller` is `"human"` or the policy's `as_str()` name (single-sourced from `RolloutPolicy`, never hand-typed); **`observation` is `"view"` or `"full-state-diagnostic"`, single-sourced from the driver constructor that built the seat** (map §1.1's label riding the artifact — lens-caught gap); fractional params in **fixed-point ppm `i64`** (Eq-safe — critic #1; `retreat_ppm` is already integral). Version semantics stated: `schema` bumps only on a `RunProvenance` shape change that breaks readers (defaulted additions do not); `SeatProvenance.version` is the policy implementation version under a stable name, bumped when a policy's sampling SEMANTICS change. A name+params map, not a typed enum: serde rejects unknown enum variants, which would make every future policy a hard deserialize error in older binaries — the opposite of ledger row 7's replay-compat intent (M2, with the trade stated).
- **Replay-inert, pinned executably:** mutate provenance on a strict-clean record → `replay_strict` still succeeds with the same terminal state; an old-shape JSON (no `run` key) still replays strict-clean; provenance never enters `state_digest` or `rules_hash` (non-goal stated). A pure record-format addition under ledger row 7 — **not** a `SEMANTICS_REV` bump, with M2's executed serde evidence cited.

**v2 ADDENDUM (2026-07-26, Task 13) — deviations recorded at execution, folded back.** This section records; it does not re-litigate. Each item shipped differently from the v2 text above, each was adopted at its task's independent review with the ground stated there, and each is written here so the spec and the code agree at the close rather than at the next reader's expense.

1. **`ArenaAgent`'s play signature (§4).** Specced as `choose_move(&mut self, view: &PlayerView, handle: &DecisionHandle, rules: &Rules) -> Move`; shipped as **`choose(&mut self, view: &PlayerView, rules: &Rules) -> Result<DecisionReport, String>`**, plus a provenance method with **deliberately no default implementation**. Three grounds. The `Result` is §4's own "sampler failure is a typed HARD error" requirement made expressible — an infallible signature has nowhere to put that failure except a panic or a silent fallback, and the silent fallback is precisely the `TrivialHonestAgent` trap the spec names. `DecisionReport` rather than a bare `Move` is what lets the driver's honest-play counters and the volume metric read what the agent actually did (§12 gate 1 asserts sampled decisions == applied moves per seat, which a bare `Move` cannot support). Dropping `&DecisionHandle` from the parameter list is the K>1 door the spec asked to keep open: the agent builds its own handle over its own determinized world, so an implementation sampling several worlds does not have to ignore a handle built over one. The `no default impl` on provenance is the T8 review's addition — a default would let an agent nobody thought about play into the corpus under a generic label.

2. **difftest's `pick_move` arm (§2).** Specced as returning a typed error for `FamilyWeighted`; shipped as a **defended arm that is unreachable from every subcommand**, because difftest's CLI parser accepts exactly its two policy names. The distinction matters and is recorded rather than glossed: the arm exists to keep the `match` exhaustive against a core enum that now has three variants, and its unreachability is a property of the PARSER, not of the type — so the parser's two-name restriction is itself pinned. Streams, sweeps, and the three checked-in artifacts' recorded figures stay exactly reproducible, which was the requirement.

3. **`Uniform`'s two-stage draw collapsed into ONE draw (§2).** Specced as a two-stage draw weighted by exact family sizes; shipped as a single `rng.gen_range(0..total)` whose result doubles as the within-routes index. Exactly uniform over the union, and the ground is stated in-source because it is a real precondition rather than a convenience: `j` is uniform on `0..routes_len` conditional on `j < routes_len` **precisely because the routes block occupies the CONTIGUOUS PREFIX `[0, routes_len)` of the draw range** — a non-contiguous mapping would need different index arithmetic (T5 review, probe 1). The prefix property is not assumed; it is pinned by `handle_routes_order_matches_the_legal_lists_non_retreat_prefix`, which asserts the handle's routes order equals the generator's non-retreat prefix, so the two implementations cannot drift apart silently.

4. **T7's interval band is χ²-derived, not binomial (§0-R3(iii)).** The spec says the real-game replicate block's band is "derived from that block's R (binomial CI stated)". Shipped **χ²-derived**, because the statistic being banded is a **ratio of two standard deviations**, not a count of successes — a binomial CI is the wrong sampling distribution for it and would have produced a band that is merely plausible-looking. Landed as R=100 × 20 pairs with empirical sd(θ̂) 0.0756 against reported SE 0.0740, ratio 1.021 inside a χ²-derived 3σ band [0.787, 1.213]. The spec's INTENT — a band derived from that block's own R, stated rather than eyeballed — is met; only the distribution changed.

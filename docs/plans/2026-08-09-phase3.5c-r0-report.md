## 1. Headline findings, each anchored to its tension

**F1 — the early vector is nearly uninformative in the regime that ships (T4, sharpened).** EVAL TOTAL early reads **0.5314 [0.5048, 0.5577]** under lookahead self-play — barely distinguishable from chance — against 0.6441 under greedy (rev-5: 0.6414). The best early member (`assistants_in_hand_excess`, 0.6910) beats the whole vector by ~0.16 AUC in the shipped regime. T4 said the early vector mixes its terms in the wrong proportions; R0-L says that in the regime that matters the mix is nearly self-cancelling.

**F2 — two early terms are CONTRADICTED under lookahead play (new evidence class).** `victory_points` EARLY 0.4651 [0.4392, 0.4921] at w_e=+10, and `assistants_on_board` EARLY 0.4464 [0.4216, 0.4711] at w_e=+14 — both interval-entirely-below-0.5 with POSITIVE weights, both confirmed-positive under greedy at both revs. No greedy row at either rev was ever contradicted. Interpretation discipline: these are correlations under one agent's self-play, not causal strategy truths — but a term whose early sign the shipped regime reverses is priced wrong for that regime by the same evidential standard that set V1's weights.

**F3 — the stage proxy is mis-calibrated for the shipped regime (new; touches the interpolation machinery itself).** Fixed-cut accuracy 0.5090 under lookahead vs 0.6757 under greedy (base rate ~0.34). Lookahead games run HALF the length (median 38 turns vs 80), so the four clocks race ahead of the post-hoc terciles: 23,012 of 26,121 true-MID turn starts classify pLATE, and the best in-sample cuts sit at 600000/800000 against the fixed 333333/666666. **F4 — the recovery statistic's rarity is a GREEDY property, not a game property (T10 sharpened).** Nonzero-recovery share 0.004655 under lookahead — **129×** greedy's 0.000036. The falsifier's 312-game sample agony was an artifact of measuring the statistic in the sparsest regime; the gate-15 demotion stands on per-edit-wall grounds regardless.

**F5 — the ChurchTrackFull monoculture is now 5-for-5 across every observed regime (T13 strengthened).** Greedy rev-7: 1998/2000 (99.90%, plus OrderDeckEmpty 2).

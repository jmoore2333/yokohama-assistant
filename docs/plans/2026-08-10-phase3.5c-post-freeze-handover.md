Because both the rules and weights changed, this was recorded-not-reopening evidence, not a claim that the original field was literally reproduced. Do **not** reopen MCTS simply because S1 is shallow; require new measured reversal evidence. See the [freeze package, OD-3](2026-08-10-phase3.5c-freeze-package.md?plain=1#L41-L43).

Approving now is also the correct stopping discipline. The fixed R0 corpus has already served as a development/selection set across multiple candidate grids. ## 4. Why the post-freeze route must be discussed now

### 4.1 The formal ladder claim and the mission claim are not yet the same

The project mission is stronger than “beats random”: the player must use country sets, opponent positions, end-game timing, and a few moves of lookahead, and must **visibly play purposefully**. See [`guidingpath.md:14`](../../guidingpath.md?plain=1#L14). The Phase-3 map makes opponent-denial and end-timing depth explicit 3.5 contents, then makes the four-part gate the evidence for the headline claim. See the [Phase 3.5 map](2026-07-25-phase3-map.md?plain=1#L165-L169).

The current evidence robustly answers “is V2 stronger than V1 inside today's controller?” It does not yet answer the whole mission construct:

- S1 is real multi-move search **within the current player's turn**: it completes the root seat's turn and evaluates the first hand-off. That correctly connects Placement to a later AreaAction, so it is not one-ply. A hand-off is nevertheless a leaf and is never expanded into the opponent's reply. See [`lookahead.rs:5–21`](../../crates/yokohama-core/src/game/lookahead.rs#L5-L21) and [`complete_turn`](../../crates/yokohama-core/src/game/lookahead.rs#L1163-L1265).
- The evaluator is statically opponent-relative, but S1 is not opponent-**response**-aware. “Block now or the opponent takes a track majority or fires a trigger next turn” can be acted on only through the hand-off proxy. A cross-handoff probe can diagnose whether that proxy produces the required behavior; only a production change would add response-aware capability.
- The freeze package itself records the missing grain: forcing the end sooner is not in the function; ahead and behind at the trigger read identically because no deficit-keyed term exists; D3 neutrality depends on recovery beyond the evaluated grain. See the [canon countersign table](2026-08-10-phase3.5c-freeze-package.md?plain=1#L45-L58).
- Round 3's genuine new signal, `achievement_race_proximity`, improved over accumulated progress early/mid but was deferred to V3. `port_empty_slots` was structurally unmeasurable at turn-start snapshots in the incumbent ecology, while `clock_control_church` was measurable but duplicated the shipped church signal's shape. Those were honest dispositions, not proof that the strategic need disappeared. See the [round-3 disposition](2026-08-09-phase3.5c-round3-evidence.md?plain=1#L7-L19).

This does **not** pre-decide that V3 or deeper search must land before the bar. It means the missing behavior must be tested before the existing formal ingredients are allowed to imply it.

### 4.2 The scenario suite is a strong regression contract, not yet a strength court

The existing scenarios should remain and continue to accumulate. Their targeted counterfactuals and never-delete discipline are valuable. But their own documentation draws the boundary:

- 20 of 21 scenarios are evaluator-ordering properties over constructed views/states;
- only the null-placement trap is a genuinely behavioral move-selection test;
- several views are deliberately doctored and engine-unreachable;
- the deck-clock case is an eval ordering, not a driven pick;
- no scenario makes a decision about firing the building trigger.

See [`scenario_suite.rs:151–199`](../../crates/yokohama-core/tests/scenario_suite.rs#L151-L199). Green here proves the evaluator preserves named arithmetic/canon preferences. It does not by itself prove that search selects an opponent-aware or conditional end-timing move, survives the opponent's reply, or sustains a plan through a whole game.

The freeze review's printed-VP catch strengthens this distinction: the review process worked and fixed the fixtures, but three control clauses/inequalities inverted before reconstruction. “21 scenarios green” must therefore be reported as 21 audited regression specifications, not 21 independent demonstrations of strong play. See the [freeze review findings](2026-08-10-phase3.5c-freeze-package.md?plain=1#L60-L62).

### 4.3 The evidence ecology is narrow and regime-dependent

R0-L is large and useful—2,000 honest lookahead self-play games—but it is not strategically diverse:

- **1,984/2,000** games ended `ChurchTrackFull`;
- lookahead median game length was **38 turns** vs greedy's 80;
- the fixed stage-proxy cuts read only **0.5090 accuracy**, with 23,012 of 26,121 true-MID turn starts classified late;
- V2 consequently spends much of the shipped regime blended toward the LATE vector.

See the [R0 headline findings](2026-08-09-phase3.5c-r0-report.md?plain=1#L7-L23). V2 repriced successfully around this ecology; it did not repair the stage mechanism. A later beam/sampling/configuration change can change the trajectory distribution again, so any accepted W6 change needs a fresh regime check rather than an assumption that the b=4 evidence transfers.

The three bridged games do not provide the missing external strength benchmark. The owner corrected the record: all three were same-owner, non-optimal solo play and are low-priority comparison inputs. See the [scope ruling](2026-08-09-phase3.5c-scope-confirmation.md?plain=1#L130-L132). Keep the comparison-only fence; do not turn those records into tuning labels merely because stronger external data is absent.

### 4.4 W7 is necessary under the map, but insufficient by itself

The planned five-rung field is uniform · retreat-averse · family-weighted · greedy-v1@V1 · lookahead-v1@V1. It is a sound preserved-rung regression ladder with paired seeds, seat swaps, fixed N, null controls, and a conjunctive pass rule. The three policy cells are expected sweeps; the V1 evaluator/search comparisons carry the discriminating weight. See the [original W7 design](2026-08-05-phase3.5-scoping-proposal.md?plain=1#L144-L161).

Therefore:

- a W7 pass can validly establish “the final contestant is superior to every preserved rung under the pre-registered field”;
- it cannot alone establish the broader purposeful-play construct, because the field contains three randomized policy families plus two internal V1 evaluator/search baselines, not an independent strategic opponent or tactical ground truth;
- the policy sweeps should be reported as regression confirmations, not as new ceiling evidence;
- the final pre-registration must include an explicit attempt-accounting policy. The old route “work, then fresh arm-2 re-attempt” has no stated limit and must not become run-until-pass.

### 4.5 The previous handover's W7-before-W6 recommendation is mechanically wrong for final proof

The original scoping order was sound: screen b/m, choose the production configuration, freeze it, then preregister W7. W7's subject is explicitly **one frozen production contestant**, with nothing moving after preregistration. See the [W6 process](2026-08-05-phase3.5-scoping-proposal.md?plain=1#L136-L140) and [W7 subject/rule](2026-08-05-phase3.5-scoping-proposal.md?plain=1#L144-L156).

The superseded revision of this handover recommended W7 at shipped knobs followed by W6. That protects the old cell from tuning contamination, but it does not protect **contestant identity**: if W6 changes a behavioral knob, W7 proved the old policy. The corrected rule is:

- W7 before W6 may run only as a clearly labelled diagnostic baseline and must be rerun for any changed final contestant; or
- preferably, use separate W6 development fields, freeze the final configuration, then run final W7 once on untouched fields.

The second is the recommended starting point for discussion. It restores the original design and avoids spending the formal ceremony twice.

**Recommendation:** run development/falsification work, not a new promotion ceremony:

3. a small bounded set of **reachable, driven** search-choice fixtures or rooted continuations—not merely doctored `score(A) > score(B)` pairs—covering:
   - opponent-progress awareness in a genuinely exclusive track race: one rooted position where altering the opponent's next-turn majority/race outcome is demonstrated worthwhile, plus a negative control where it is not; do not encode denial as a default strategy;
   - accelerate or complete one's own achievement leg when proximity matters, with no opponent-denial claim (both seats may claim the same achievement);
   - fire/accelerate an end trigger where the full score and true remaining-turn count support it, paired with a behind-state control that should keep building efficiently rather than encode a generic brake; pin current-player/start-player position because the trigger round must finish and then one full final round is played;

Where practical, force alternative root moves and compare downstream results, or play the rooted state to terminal. A result under one fixed continuation policy is **policy-conditional**, not action ground truth; where the opponent reply is material, use a small named set of plausible/adversarial reply policies, an exact bounded solve, or robustness across named continuations. The purpose is to falsify action quality and horizon assumptions.

Pilot first. Before any driven family supports a positive GO or OD-11 reading, freeze its reachable root(s), legal alternative set, expected move/top-set or downstream-dominance criterion, negative control, seed/world replication, and pass/fail rule. If a population interval is to be claimed, size N from observed variance rather than inventing a gate-sized constant. Findings and resolutions go to PR comments. The owner-facing scoping amendment after merge is a design/adjudication task, not an implementation dispatch.

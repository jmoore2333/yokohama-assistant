## Architecture

Workspace crates: `crates/yokohama-core` (library), `crates/yokohama-cli` (game driver), `crates/yokohama-oracle` (independent rules oracle), `crates/yokohama-difftest` (differential harness), `crates/yokohama-bridge` (BGA capture relay), `crates/gen-data` (YAML→Rust generator). **`yokohama-bridge` depends on NO engine crate and never will** — a localhost-only HTTP sink appending what the browser extension in `bridge/extension/` observes to `bridge-captures/<table_id>.ndjson`; it knows no rules and parses no move — translation is `yokohama-cli`'s `import`, deliberately across that boundary so a capture can never be corrupted by a rules bug. The browser half is read-only toward BGA by construction (copies responses the page already received; issues zero requests of its own). CLI public modules: `describe`/`render`/`repl`/`screen`/`session`/`style`/`wizard` + crate-private `input`/`reveal`/`why` (and `bot`/`suggest`), over a thin `main` — `screen` composes the frame and owns `visible_width` (the escape-stripping measurement every width guard uses); `style` owns ALL ANSI knowledge (identity until `init_from_tty`; `NO_COLOR` honored) and the alignment rule: pad from the PLAIN text — escapes add bytes with zero display width.

- `types/` — serde-serializable state. `GameState` is complete-and-resumable and **history-free by design** (cheap to clone for search; move history belongs in an external action log, never in state). `Move`/`AreaAction` in `types/moves.rs`; typed `MoveError` in `types/errors.rs`.
- `game/` — pure functions over state: `get_legal_moves(&GameState, &Rules) -> Vec<Move>`, `apply_move(...) -> Result<GameState, MoveError>` (clones, never mutates). **`apply_move` is the CHECKED boundary** — it rejects any move not in the current legal list; `apply_move_unchecked` is the internal fast path (moves already drawn from the list). `Rules::standard()` from generated registries; `Rules::empty_for_tests()` for fixtures; `Rules::content_hash()` stamps `GameRecord.rules_hash` (`replay` warns on mismatch; `replay_strict` adds per-event invariant checks — the training-record path — and verifies the terminal digest). Seeded `new_game(...) -> PendingSetup` + `finalize_setup`; decks live IN `GameState` (`order_deck`/`tech_deck`, last = top; `orders_removed` for conservation); `place_setup_dummies` is `pub` (Phase-3 primitive for a valid 2P state outside `new_game`).
- `game/search_actions.rs` — the FACTORED bot-facing action source, families-first: `families()` names the legal families; `family_len`/`iter_family`/`sample_family` page or sample WITHIN one (`sample_family -> Option<Move>`: `None` on a structurally-empty family — `families()` deliberately emits the tag without probing emptiness). **THE THREE FAMILY-COST SHAPES DIFFER — never transfer one family's language to another:** *retreat* LAZY serve-one (mixed-radix index; `family_len` exact closed form); *placement* LAZY generate-and-filter (`family_len` an honest UPPER bound — `iter_family` is the authority on realized moves); *routes* COMPUTE-PER-CALL, small but exact, no cache — a caller needing count AND moves pages the iterator rather than asking twice (L10). Small families delegate to the exhaustive generators — equivalent by construction. **`get_legal_moves` stays the authority**; these modules are purely additive and are what search calls.
- `game/route_enum.rs` — the exact allocation-free President-route enumerator: reproduces the frozen `enumerate_president_routes` EXACTLY (same pairs, order, lex-least witness); `movement_routes_family` assembles the WHOLE Movement family as `legal_moves.rs` does. The win is allocation shape, not complexity (the label is three scalars, so the DFS carries `(u8, u32)` over fixed arrays; worst-case TIME is still `O(affordable simple paths)`). Totality is a PRE-FLIGHT probe — `fits_fixed_arrays` checks all bounds BEFORE any write; a failing state (hand-built fixtures only) delegates to the frozen path, never panics. The routes family is SMALL — **max OBSERVED 694 adversarial / 66 organic under the shipped policy (policy-conditional, L11), NO label ceiling known (L9): cite "max observed under harness X", never a bound.**
- `game/move_legality.rs` — single-candidate legality for a search that PROPOSES a move: `is_move_legal(state, mv, rules, mode)` (exhaustive match, no `_` arm — a new variant is a compile error, not a silent rejection) + `is_president_path_legal`. Re-stated cheap per-arm predicates (design A, won by measurement; apply-probing is far slower AND unsound alone — raw arms defend only structure and never read `turn_phase`). `PathMode::RulesLegal` accepts any rules-legal WITNESS — deliberately WIDER than list membership with ZERO new labels, so **equivalence tests compare `MovePresident` by `(destination, RouteVector)` LABEL, never raw path**; `PathMode::Canonical` adds only the generator's teleport suppression and does NOT verify lex-least witness selection. ZERO narrowings vs `get_legal_moves` since rev 4: the Canal exclusion is single-sourced in the two shared movement predicates (`is_possible_action_area_full`, `can_step_onto`) so generator, predicates, both route searches, and apply agree BY CONSTRUCTION (pinned by `the_canal_is_excluded_by_construction_across_generator_predicate_and_apply`).
- **3.2 — the arena and the policy rungs** (`game/seeds.rs`, `game/metrics.rs`, `game/policy.rs`, `game/arena.rs`, + `DecisionHandle`/`FamilySize`). `RolloutPolicy` = the three preserved rungs: `Uniform`, `RetreatAverse` (never volunteer a retreat), `FamilyWeighted { retreat_ppm, recovery_min_of }` (defaults 166,667 ppm, k=5; FULL SUPPORT at every recovery vector — the defect it fixes). **Sampler-side ONLY: the engine's legal list is never weighted.** Lives in core so the fuzzer reaches it. `DecisionHandle` = the enumerate-once handle (L10 as a property of a type); its DFS-counter pin is **harness-scoped, never globally armed** (the CLI bot path legitimately pays 2 in debug) and **structurally blind at in-hand Movement points** — hence the separate per-edit pick allocation bound with its own in-hand cell. `FamilySize::{Exact, UpperBound}` keeps placement's upper bound from being silently summed into exact sizes. The arena defines its OWN `ArenaAgent` seam (not `HonestAgent`, whose `choose_move` takes a materialized `&[Move]` — the ceiling the driver exists to avoid); honest by construction (`observe` → `BeliefSampler` K=1 → `DecisionHandle` → `pick` → `is_move_legal` → `apply_move_unchecked`); sampler failure is a **typed hard error with NO fallback arm** (`TrivialHonestAgent` silently falls back to the TRUE legal list — an arena inheriting that would emit privileged-play games into the corpus). **Seeds are type-enforced**: the public surface takes `WorldSeed`, never bare `u64`; masters from OS entropy per evaluation run, recorded; `SeedSchedule::diagnostic(master)` is the reproduction path and its NAME is the waiver; **no value-based seed checks anywhere** — the enforced property is the DERIVATION. `GameRecord.run` carries `RunProvenance`/`SeatProvenance` (replay-inert); corpus layout `rev<N>/<players>P-<cell>/<seed:016x>-<arr>.json`.
- **3.4 — the shared budget and the MCTS contestant** (`game/budget.rs`, `game/mcts.rs`, the `cap` arm in `game/lookahead.rs`). `ScoredBudget` = the ONE currency both architectures spend: **TOTAL scored successors per decision, root pass INCLUDED** — a deterministic COUNT, never wall-clock/entropy, so a decision reproduces from its master. S1 cap: opt-in, byte-inert when absent; root pass mandatory; continuations ATOMIC in descending one-ply-value order with a keyed tie draw; starved decisions fall back keyed-uniform over the root family, never first-listed. `mcts-v1`: a fresh UCT tree per decision over ONE sampled world (**`K > 1` is REFUSED at the search** — aggregating across worlds is information-set search, out of 3.4 on measured grounds), one `DecisionHandle` per node, UCB1 over a declared [0,1] squash, keyed draws at every tie/pick, terminal values from `final_result`, `LeafRule::HandOff` default + two labelled diagnostics; the module doc carries the FIVE-item honesty contract and the leakage-vs-world-sensitivity distinction (leakage ASSERTED zero; world-sensitivity RECORDED, scale uncalibrated). **MCTS-v1 was measured and NOT adopted** (`docs/plans/2026-08-04-phase3.4-architecture-decision.md`) — kept reachable as a labelled diagnostic, metered direction-free.
- **Phases B/C — the driven-fixture harness + family layer** (`game/driven.rs`, `game/driven_runner.rs`, `game/driven_families.rs`, `examples/driven.rs`; designs/plans `docs/plans/2026-08-17-phase-b-harness-*.md` — amended IN PLACE; read the amended text — and `2026-08-18-phase-c-families-*`). Rooted runs over the honest chain: the arena's game loop was SPLIT behavior-preservingly (byte-verified) into a setup half and a **RECORD-FREE play half** — no `GameRecord` is ever constructed inside `driven.rs`; a rooted run must not masquerade as a from-setup game. `root_table` MIRRORS `Contestant::build`'s arms (drift-pinned; **lookahead-only** — `GreedyAgent` has no ranked surface, and a greedy table would need a second evaluation derivation, forbidden). `adjudicate_root` forces every root candidate, plays remainders K times under named reply seats; **a playout failure REFUSES THE WHOLE TABLE** (a skipped row would read as fewer options); margins ride `final_result`'s official standings (best rival by RANK; margin 0 is not a win). `RootSource` = Constructed / RecordPrefix via `state_at` / Generated (**the GENERATING ECOLOGY is recorded — predicate+master+ordinal alone is not an identity**); `driven::registry()` ships the six owner-game roots with verified decision seeds + recorded moves; the loader transcription-checks with typed `Stale` (a rev drift must never present as a separator divergence). Phase C's layer 2: `driven_families.rs` (turn predicates over hand-off states, the measured-shape DEAD classifier, splitting-rule admission arithmetic with the lookahead-sole-dissenter guard, `family_read` under its own salt, three parameterized builders, the FOUR frozen family declarations with baked `FrozenBlock`s, `run_family_block`), `drive_turn` (dual stop: `current_player` change OR game over), `ReplyPolicy::RaceRusher` (the declared adversarial reply seat over its own derived probe world). **Off the fifteen-gate roster; tests corpus-free** (proven green with the saves hidden); a graduating fixture joins `scenario_suite`, never this module.
- A turn is a phase state machine (`TurnPhase`): AdditionalActionFirst → Placement → Movement → AreaAction → FivePowerBonus → Building → Recovery (automatic) → AdditionalActionSecond → TurnEnd. Mercenary use runs a nested sub-turn (AreaAction→…→Recovery) — see `is_mercenary_action`.
- Hidden information — READ THE DISTINCTION, it is load-bearing for Phase 3. **In the engine's REPRESENTATION** exactly one thing is hidden player-to-player: each player's starting order (`starting_order` / `starting_order_hidden`; both dealt cards sit in `orders_removed`; reveals via `extract_order_from_deck`). **From a SEATED PLAYER's view that is NOT the whole hidden set** — a bot handed a raw `GameState` is cheating: the state also stores the exact future `order_deck`/`tech_deck` ORDER and the boxed `orders_removed` pile (at 2P the boxing is largest — the unknown is largest exactly where the first strength milestone is). Those fields are correct for replay/self-play; they are not an observation. **Phase 3.0 built the boundary**: `types/view.rs` + `game/observe.rs` (masks the FIVE-member hidden set — the four above plus `GameConfig.setup_seed`) + `game/belief.rs` (`BeliefSampler`, conservation-consistent determinization) + `game/agent.rs`. **A bot takes a `PlayerView`, never a raw `GameState`.** Masking the seed does not fully close the channel — the public board fingerprints the seed (the seed-oracle residual, closed operationally by full-`u64` recorded seeds; map §8). The live-tracking path treats the seeded deck/removed split as fiction until physical cards are revealed — the same fact from the other side.
- Static card data: source of truth `analysis-1/yokohama-kb/07..09-*.yaml` (verified: 36 orders keyed by printed corner index, 20 tech titles × `copies:` = 30 physical cards, 24 sites with TL/TR/ML/MR `SpaceReward` spaces). `gen-data` emits `game/data.rs`.
- Boards (verified from board art): ports SIX card slots (2 per LOW/MID/HIGH), labs SIX tech slots (surcharges +1,+1,+2,+2 on slots 3–6), customs 7 spaces with printed costs [1,1,2,2,3,3,4], church faith 4–11.
- Area-board topology (`board_gen`): a STAGGERED PYRAMID, widest row on top — rows 4/3/2/1 (2P) · 5/4/3/2 (3P) · 6/5/4/3 (4P), brick-offset adjacency (≤6 neighbors), undirected edge counts pinned 18/28/38, any-to-any Canal — NOT a rectangular grid. `BoardLayout.adjacency` is the single graph all consumers read; no row-metadata on `GameState`. The two renderers get rows differently, deliberately: `render::board_grid` (LIVE board) groups purely by `AreaConfig::grid_position` and assumes no shape (any geometry renders — it cannot drift from the data); `board_gen::row_lengths(pc)` serves only where there is no state to read (`board_skeleton`, wizard tile-entry), pinned by `board_skeleton_shape_matches_row_lengths_at_every_player_count`.

## Rules knowledge

- Complete reference: `analysis-1/yokohama-kb/01-rules-reference.md` (corrected as verification finds errors — the decision log lists corrections); rulebook PDF in `analysis-1/`; icon glossary rulebook p.10.
- Commonly-missed rules that have bitten this project: Trading House passive income (owner gets 1 Yen when another player acts in that area); movement-icon rewards move *assistants* via queued declinable choices — there is NO 5-Power "Movement token" (box-verified: no Movement or VP faces in the 20-token pool); mercenaries bypass opponent Presidents (no blocking, no fees); the President may not return to its starting area in the same turn; placement fees are 1 Yen PER ASSISTANT PLACED to each president owner (Option B's 2-into-one-area costs 2, not 1); movement pass-through fees are paid TO the president owners (1 Yen each), not the bank; Trading House and Shophouse both require Power 4+ (no separate higher gate for Trading House); a player may only own 1 Technology card of the same name (10 of the 20 titles circulate as 2 physical copies so different players can each own one); trading-house tokens go warehouse→hand at the Employment Agency (printed 4/5/6/7 take progression) and build FREE from hand; port refill is rightmost-first after total left-compaction; Port at 5 Power also grants +3 VP; Chinatown exchange count is capped by action power; Movement Option B is legal with the President already in hand; the 2P dummy pawn (church faith-7 / customs space-4) is a real scoring occupant that COMPETES in the church (6/3) and customs (8/4) majority bonuses — not merely an eligibility block (finding D1, rulebook p.9 NOTE — **Jeremy's edition only**, NOT the English PDF in `analysis-1/`; authoritative text: the archived 2026-07-21 rows + `docs/plans/2026-07-20-phase-2.9-ruling-list.md` §D1).
- **The five BGA-parity rulings (2026-08-08, revs 6–7) change rules the bullets above and below describe — read them together:** the Church yen payment costs **2 Yen** (D1); the extra-technology surcharge starts at the **4th** card (D2); a movement icon may return an assistant to the owner's HAND, where it stays (D3); the 2P dummy competes in the Technology majority at industry value **7.5** (D5 — the engine compares doubled values, so a tie against the dummy is unrepresentable); the Church/Customs track pawn is sourced from the ACTIVATED AREA first, hand only as fallback (D6). **D3 and D6 are deliberate parity divergences from this project's own reading**, adopted so bridged BGA games replay through this engine, the disagreement RECORDED rather than resolved. Authority: the archived 2026-08-08 rows — read those, not this summary, before touching the affected code.
- Church faith = power LINEARLY (1,2,3,4,5) plus 1 per distinct payment type (max 1 each of copper/silk/tea/seafood/yen/import). The rulebook's "2 Power: 1 Faith" is a TYPO (Jeremy-adjudicated); `calculate_church_faith` is corrected.
- Choice encoding — every former v1 auto-resolution is a real queued choice: any-good picks (no decline — gain is unconditional), warehouse-icon takes (Decline always offered; 4/5/6/7 pricing), movement-icon assistant moves (Decline per icon; D3's `MoveTarget { Hand, Area }`), church/customs space choice (queued only when ≥2 eligible — exactly one places inline), Telephone's Recovery leave-one (may keep a LONE assistant — recovers zero at count 1), Station placement (engine-authority: every non-Canal area eligible), University/Spinning Mill as standing additional-action options. Singleton resolution auto-resolves silently; for every DECLINABLE choice that singleton is structurally a no-op Decline (pinned invariant — CLI delta windows depend on it); `StationPlace` is the documented exception — no Decline at all, so a lone eligible area places INLINE with a real effect (design-sanctioned, unreachable on any generated board, pinned in `choices.rs` and scoped by `choice_encoding.rs`'s `assert_decline_or_never_singleton`).
- Canal (ruled ANY-TO-ANY on the staggered board — supersedes the earlier colinear model; archived 2026-07-21 rows): the Canal connects ANY of its adjacent tiles to ANY other for President movement; 1 Yen per crossing to the BANK (not a player). The tolled `canal_crossings` SET is only the neighbor pairs NOT already joined by a free brick-offset edge (a directly-adjacent pair keeps its free edge; the tolled transit is legal but dominated, self-resolving). The Canal itself is never placeable/stoppable — single-sourced in the two shared movement predicates (rev 4), with the Station-teleport and mercenary-target arms gated at their own sites (rev 5) — and Telegram's adjacent-bonus placement excludes crossing pairs (transit-only adjacency). Derivation single-sourced in `board_gen::derive_canal_crossings`, reused by `reconcile_board`.
